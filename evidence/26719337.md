# Recruitment of Mcm10 to Sites of Replication Initiation Requires Direct Binding to the Minichromosome Maintenance (MCM) Complex

- **PMID**: 26719337
- **Authors**: Max E. Douglas, John F. X. Diffley
- **Journal**: Journal of Biological Chemistry (2016) 291:5879-5888
- **DOI**: 10.1074/jbc.M115.707802
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26719337/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N16` (`MCM10` → `MCM2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates the molecular mechanism by which Mcm10, an essential factor for eukaryotic DNA replication initiation and activation of the CMG (Cdc45-MCM-GINS) helicase, is recruited to sites of replication initiation. Analyses using purified proteins demonstrate that Mcm10 binds directly to both DNA-bound MCM double hexamers and soluble MCM single hexamers via its C-terminal region, with the binding site on MCM involving the Mcm2 and Mcm6 subunits. Mcm10 mutants defective in direct MCM binding fail in both modes of origin recruitment (low-affinity G1-like binding and high-affinity binding during S-phase CMG assembly) and are unable to support DNA replication or CMG helicase activation.

# Retinoblastoma protein recruits histone deacetylase to repress transcription

- **Journal**: Nature (1998) 391:597-601

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The retinoblastoma tumor suppressor protein (Rb) binds to E2F family transcription factors and arrests cell proliferation by actively repressing the transcription of genes required for the transition from G1 to S phase. In this study, Rb was shown to physically interact with the histone deacetylase HDAC1 via its pocket domain and recruit HDAC1 to E2F target promoters. Rb and HDAC1 cooperate to repress E2F-regulated promoter activity, and this repression is relieved by the histone deacetylase inhibitor trichostatin A (TSA), tumor-derived Rb pocket mutations, or the HPV E7 protein.

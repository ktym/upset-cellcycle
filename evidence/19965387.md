# Phosphorylation of H2A by Bub1 prevents chromosomal instability through localizing shugoshin

- **PMID**: 19965387
- **Authors**: Shigehiro A. Kawashima, Yuya Yamagishi, Takashi Honda, Kei-ichiro Ishiguro, Yoshinori Watanabe
- **Journal**: Science (2010) 327:172-177
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1180189
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19965387/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N23` (`BUB1` → `SGO1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

During mitosis and meiosis, the kinetochore kinase Bub1 is required for the centromeric recruitment of Shugoshin (Sgo1/Sgo2), which mediates centromeric cohesin protection and chromosomal passenger complex (CPC) localization, yet the critical substrate of Bub1 had long remained unknown. Using chemical genetics and phosphoproteomic analyses, this study discovered that Bub1 directly phosphorylates a conserved C-terminal serine/threonine residue of histone H2A (Ser121 in fission yeast; Thr120 in humans, H2A-T120). Mutating this Bub1-mediated H2A phosphorylation site to a non-phosphorylatable form abolishes the centromeric localization of Shugoshin and causes chromosomal instability (CIN), proving that H2A-T120 phosphorylation by Bub1 is key to localizing Shugoshin to centromeres and ensuring accurate chromosome segregation.

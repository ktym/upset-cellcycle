## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Polo-like kinase 1 (PLK1) is a key regulator of mitotic progression and is particularly essential for recovery from DNA damage checkpoint arrest in G2 phase (checkpoint recovery), yet how PLK1 is initially activated prior to mitotic entry remained unclear. This study demonstrates that the mitotic kinase Aurora A (AURKA), aided by its cofactor Bora, directly phosphorylates a conserved threonine residue (Thr210) within the T-loop of PLK1 during G2 phase to activate PLK1. Furthermore, this Thr210 phosphorylation and activation of PLK1 by Aurora A and Bora is shown to be essential for recovery from DNA damage checkpoint-mediated G2 arrest and subsequent entry into mitosis.

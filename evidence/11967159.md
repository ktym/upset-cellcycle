# Identification of Tah11/Sid2 as the ortholog of the replication licensing factor Cdt1 in Saccharomyces cerevisiae

- **PMID**: 11967159
- **Authors**: Alain Devault, Elizabeth A. Vallen, Tina Yuan, Stephen Green, Aaron Bensimon, Etienne Schwob
- **Journal**: Current Biology (2002) 12:689-694
- **DOI**: 10.1016/s0960-9822(02)00768-6
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11967159/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-022` (`TAH11` → `MCM2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

While Cdt1 was known in fission yeast and metazoans as an essential DNA replication licensing factor required for loading the Mcm2-7 helicase complex onto chromatin, its ortholog in budding yeast had long remained unknown. This study identified the budding yeast *TAH11* (*SID2* / *CDT1*) gene product as the functional and structural ortholog of Cdt1. Tah11/Cdt1 interacts with the Mcm2-7 complex and is essential for loading Mcm2 (the Mcm2-7 complex) onto replication origin chromatin in cooperation with Cdc6 during G1 phase (licensing), and cells depleted of Tah11 were shown to completely lose chromatin binding of Mcm2 and initiation of DNA replication.

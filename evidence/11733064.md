# Regulation of the Bub2/Bfa1 GAP complex by Cdc5 and cell cycle checkpoints

- **PMID**: 11733064
- **Authors**: Hu F, Wang Y, Liu D, Li Y, Qin J, Elledge SJ
- **Journal**: Cell (2001) 107:655-665
- **DOI**: 10.1016/s0092-8674(01)00580-3
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11733064/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-056` (`CDC5` → `BFA1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In the budding yeast mitotic exit network (MEN), the GTPase Tem1 is negatively regulated by the Bub2/Bfa1 two-component GAP (GTPase-activating protein) complex. This study revealed that the Polo-like kinase Cdc5 directly phosphorylates Bfa1 during M phase and inactivates the Bub2/Bfa1 complex, thereby activating the MEN and promoting mitotic exit. A non-phosphorylatable mutant of Bfa1 carrying mutations at Cdc5 phosphorylation sites (Bfa1-11A) was resistant to inhibition by Cdc5 and delayed mitotic exit. Furthermore, upon activation of the spindle assembly checkpoint or spindle position checkpoint, phosphorylation of Bfa1 by Cdc5 was prevented, maintaining Tem1 in an inactive state.

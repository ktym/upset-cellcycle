# Human Bub1 defines the persistent cohesion site along the mitotic chromosome by affecting Shugoshin localization

- **PMID**: 15723797
- **Authors**: Tomoya S. Kitajima, Silke Hauf, Miho Ohsugi, Tadashi Yamamoto, Yoshinori Watanabe
- **Journal**: Current Biology (2005) 15:353-359
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cub.2004.12.044
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15723797/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N23` (`BUB1` → `SGO1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

During mitotic prophase in vertebrates, cohesin along chromosome arms dissociates in a Polo-like kinase (Plk1)-dependent manner, whereas centromeric cohesin is protected until anaphase onset by Shugoshin (Sgo1). Through RNAi knockdown analyses in human cells, this study demonstrates that the spindle checkpoint kinase Bub1 is essential for localizing Sgo1 to centromeres. Depletion of Bub1 prevents Sgo1 accumulation at centromeres and instead causes Sgo1 and cohesin to persist along the entire length of chromosome arms, abolishing centromere-specific cohesin protection and demonstrating that Bub1 defines the centromeric localization of Sgo1.

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates the regulatory mechanisms of Mih1, the budding yeast Cdc25 homolog that promotes mitotic entry by dephosphorylating Swe1-dependent inhibitory phosphorylation on Tyr19 (Y19) of Cdk1 (Cdc28). Mih1 is hyperphosphorylated during interphase by casein kinase 1 (Yck1/Yck2), but becomes dephosphorylated by the PP2A-Cdc55 phosphatase upon entry into mitosis. Furthermore, Cdk1 (Cdc28) directly phosphorylates Mih1 and is required to initiate Mih1 dephosphorylation during mitotic entry, demonstrating that dephosphorylation and activation of Cdc28 by Mih1 and regulation of Mih1 by Cdc28 form a positive feedback loop governing the G2/M transition.

# Treslin collaborates with TopBP1 in triggering the initiation of DNA replication

- **PMID**: 20116089
- **Authors**: Akiko Kumagai, Anna Shevchenko, Andrej Shevchenko, William G. Dunphy
- **Journal**: Cell (2010) 140:349-359
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cell.2009.12.049
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20116089/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N12` (`TICRR|MTBP` → `TOPBP1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In vertebrate DNA replication initiation, the replication initiation factor TopBP1 plays an essential role, but how TopBP1 recruits Cdc45 to replication origins to initiate S phase remained unknown. Through proteomic analysis, this study identified Treslin (TICRR) as a novel TopBP1-interacting protein. Treslin binds to chromatin independently of TopBP1, and associates with TopBP1 in a CDK2-dependent manner prior to the onset of DNA replication. Immunodepletion of Treslin from Xenopus egg extracts or siRNA knockdown in human cells strongly inhibits Cdc45 loading onto chromatin and chromosomal DNA replication, proving that Treslin is an essential factor that collaborates with TopBP1 to trigger the initiation of DNA replication.

# Phosphorylation-dependent ubiquitination of cyclin E by the SCFFbw7 ubiquitin ligase

- **PMID**: 11533444
- **Authors**: Deanna M. Koepp, Laura K. Schaefer, Xin Ye, Khandan Keyomarsi, Claire Chu, J. Wade Harper, Stephen J. Elledge
- **Journal**: Science (2001) 294:173-177
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1065203
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11533444/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-029` (`FBXW7` → `CCNE1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Cyclin E (CCNE1) binds and activates Cdk2 to catalyze the transition from G1 to S phase of the cell cycle, and its intracellular protein levels are tightly regulated via degradation by the ubiquitin-proteasome system. This study identified SCF-Fbw7 (FBXW7) as the E3 ubiquitin ligase responsible for cyclin E ubiquitination and demonstrated that its function is evolutionarily conserved across yeast, *Drosophila*, and mammals. Fbw7 specifically associates with phosphorylated cyclin E, and the SCF-Fbw7 complex directly catalyzes cyclin E ubiquitination in vitro. Furthermore, depletion of Fbw7 in human and *Drosophila* cells was shown to cause stabilization and hyperaccumulation of cyclin E in vivo.

# Yeast Hct1 is a regulator of Clb2 cyclin proteolysis

- **Journal**: Cell (1997) 90:683-693
- **DOI**: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80529-2

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Cell-cycle stage-specific proteolysis of mitotic cyclins plays a fundamental role in eukaryotic cell-cycle control. In this study, the conserved eukaryotic WD40-repeat protein Hct1 (Cdh1) was discovered to be a necessary and rate-limiting regulator of the mitotic cyclin Clb2 degradation pathway in budding yeast. In hct1 mutants, the mitotic cyclin Clb2 is highly stabilized and inappropriately induces DNA replication, whereas the half-lives of G1 cyclins and other proteolytic substrates remain unaffected. Furthermore, overexpression of Hct1 ectopically induces destruction box- and APC subunit Cdc23-dependent degradation of Clb2, demonstrating that Hct1/Cdh1 functions as a substrate-specific activator of APC-dependent Clb2 proteolysis.

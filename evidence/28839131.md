# Whi7 is an unstable cell-cycle repressor of the Start transcriptional program

- **PMID**: 28839131
- **Authors**: Mercè Gomar-Alba, Ester Méndez, Inma Quilis, M. Carmen Bañó, J. Carlos Igual
- **Journal**: Nature Communications (2017) 8:329
- **DOI**: 10.1038/s41467-017-00374-1
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28839131/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N01` (`CLN3|CDC28` → `WHI7`), `SCE-CC-N02` (`WHI7` → `SWI4|SWI6`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study demonstrated that Whi7 (Srl3), a budding yeast protein with sequence similarity to Whi5, is a genuine Whi5 paralog that represses the transcriptional program at the G1/S transition (Start). Whi7 associates with the promoter regions of G1/S genes during late G1 and acts cooperatively with Whi5 as a repressor of SBF (Swi4–Swi6 complex)-dependent transcription. At the same time, Whi7 is an unstable protein that is inactivated upon phosphorylation by Cdk1 (such as Cln3–Cdc28) and targeted for degradation by the SCF-Grr1 ubiquitin ligase. Thus, much like mammalian cells, budding yeast relies on the combined action of multiple Start transcriptional repressors (Whi5 and Whi7) and their regulation via CDK phosphorylation to tightly control the G1/S transition.

- **Extracted Phrases/Sentences**:
  > "Start is the main decision point in eukaryotic cell cycle in which cells commit to a new round of cell division. It involves the irreversible activation of a transcriptional program by G1 CDK-cyclin complexes through the inactivation of Start transcriptional repressors, Whi5 in yeast or Rb in mammals. Here we provide novel keys of how Whi7, a protein related at sequence level to Whi5, represses Start.

# Transcriptional activation of CLN1, CLN2, and a putative new G1 cyclin (HCS26) by SWI4, a positive regulator of G1-specific transcription

- **PMID**: 1832336
- **Authors**: J. Ogas, B. J. Andrews, I. Herskowitz
- **Journal**: Cell (1991) 66:1015-1026
- **DOI**: 10.1016/0092-8674(91)90445-5
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1832336/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-007` (`SWI4|SWI6` → `CLN1|CLN2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Swi4, a component of the budding yeast transcription factor complex SBF (CCBF: cell cycle box factor), is essential for the proliferation of haploid cells at high temperatures and diploid (a/α) cells, and its loss causes an unbudded G1 arrest. From a screen for multicopy suppressors of the temperature sensitivity of swi4 mutants, the G1 cyclin genes CLN1 and CLN2 as well as a novel cyclin gene HCS26 (PCL1) were isolated. Transcript levels of CLN1, CLN2, and HCS26 are drastically reduced by 3- to 20-fold in swi4-deficient strains, and the promoter regions of these genes contain SBF (CCBF) binding sequences where physical binding was confirmed, demonstrating that SBF (Swi4-Swi6) directly activates the transcription of G1 cyclin genes to form a positive feedback loop during G1 phase.

# A role for the transcription factors Mbp1 and Swi4 in progression from G1 to S phase

- **PMID**: 8372350
- **Authors**: C. Koch, T. Moll, M. Neuberg, H. Ahorn, K. Nasmyth
- **Journal**: Science (1993) 261:1551-1557
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.8372350
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8372350/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-006` (`MBP1` → `SWI6`), `SCE-CC-008` (`MBP1|SWI6` → `CLB5|CLB6`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

During late G1 phase in budding yeast, transcription is activated for G1 cyclin genes (regulated by SBF via SCB sequences) as well as DNA synthesis-related genes and CLB5/CLB6 (regulated by MBF via MCB sequences). In this study, the transcription factor complex MBF, which binds to MCB sequences, was biochemically purified, and Mbp1 was identified and cloned as the 120 kDa DNA-binding subunit that forms a complex with Swi6. Mbp1 possesses a DNA-binding domain and a Swi6-binding region structurally similar to those of Swi4. While deletion of MBP1 alone is viable, it abolishes the periodicity of MCB-dependent transcription, and mbp1 swi4 double mutants arrest in late G1 and are inviable, demonstrating that transcriptional activation by MBF and SBF is essential for the transition from G1 to S phase.

- **TSV Description**: Identification of the Mbp1-Swi6 complex (MBF). The mbp1 swi4 double mutant is lethal.
- **Location**: Abstract
- **Extracted Phrases/Sentences**:
  > "A different, but related, complex called MBF binds to MCB elements (Mlu I cell cycle box) found in the promoter of most DNA synthesis genes. MBF contains Swi6 and a 120-kilodalton protein (p120). MBF was purified and the gene encoding p120 (termed MBP1) was cloned... Strains deleted for both MBP1 and SWI4 were inviable, demonstrating that transcriptional activation by MBF and SBF has an important role in the transition from G1 to S phase."
- **Evidence Explanation**: Identifies through purification and cloning that the MBF complex is composed of Mbp1 (p120) and Swi6, and directly shows that the mbp1 swi4 double mutant strain is inviable.

- **Evidence Explanation**: Supports that periodic transcription in late G1 of the S-phase cyclins CLB5/CLB6 and DNA replication genes, which harbor MCB elements in their promoters, is directly regulated by MBF (the Mbp1-Swi6 complex).

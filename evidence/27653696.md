# APC/C and SCF(cyclin F) Constitute a Reciprocal Feedback Circuit Controlling S-Phase Entry

- **PMID**: 27653696
- **Authors**: Rajarshi Choudhury, Thomas Bonacci, Anthony Arceci, Debojyoti Lahiri, Christine A. Mills, Jennifer L. Kernan, Timothy B. Branigan, James A. DeCaprio, Daniel J. Burke, Michael J. Emanuele
- **Journal**: Cell Reports (2016) 16:3359-3371
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.celrep.2016.08.058
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27653696/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N06` (`FZR1` → `CCNF`), `HSA-CC-N08` (`CCNF|SKP1|CUL1` → `FZR1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

During G1 phase, the anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) binds to its substrate receptor Cdh1 (FZR1), and APC/C-Cdh1 plays a critical role in restricting S-phase entry and maintaining genome integrity. This study uncovered a mutually antagonistic feedback circuit between APC/C-Cdh1 and a second ubiquitin ligase complex, SCF-cyclin F (SKP1-CUL1-CCNF). In G1 phase, APC/C-Cdh1 ubiquitinates the F-box protein cyclin F (CCNF) and targets it for degradation, whereas at the G1/S transition, SCF-cyclin F recognizes and degrades Cdh1 as a substrate to inactivate the APC/C and promote S-phase entry. This coordinated temporal regulation of cyclin F and Cdh1 degradation via a double-negative feedback loop constitutes a fundamental mechanism governing the timing of the G1/S transition.

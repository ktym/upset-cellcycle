- **PMID**: 9192873

- **Journal**: Genes & Development (1997) 11:1464-1478
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.11.11.1464

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-020` (`CCNE1|CDK2` → `CDKN1B`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The CDK inhibitor p27Kip1 (CDKN1B) not only inhibits cyclin-CDK complexes but also functions as a substrate that is negatively regulated by the cyclin E-CDK2 complex. In this study, cyclin E-CDK2 was demonstrated to directly phosphorylate p27Kip1 on Thr-187 (T187) in vitro and in cells, causing the elimination (degradation) of p27 from the cell and promoting the transition from G1 to S phase. A mutant substituting alanine for T187 (T187A) is resistant to cyclin E-induced degradation and causes a cyclin E-resistant G1 block.

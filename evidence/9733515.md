# Activation of the ATM kinase by ionizing radiation and phosphorylation of p53

- **PMID**: 9733515

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The tumor suppressor protein p53 is phosphorylated on Ser-15 (S15) and activated in response to various DNA-damaging stimuli. This study demonstrated that ATM, which acts upstream of p53 in a signal transduction pathway initiated by ionizing radiation, possesses intrinsic protein kinase activity and directly phosphorylates p53 on Ser-15 in a manganese-dependent manner. Exposure to ionizing radiation rapidly enhances the p53-directed kinase activity of endogenous ATM, establishing a direct molecular link from DNA double-strand breaks to p53 activation.

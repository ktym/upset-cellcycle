# Direct binding of cyclin D to the retinoblastoma gene product (pRb) and pRb phosphorylation by the cyclin D-dependent kinase CDK4

- **PMID**: 8449399

- **Journal**: Genes & Development (1993) 7:331-342
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.7.3.331

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-001` (`CCND1|CDK4|CDK6` → `RB1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The retinoblastoma gene product (pRb) prevents S-phase entry in the cell cycle, and its growth-suppressive function is inactivated by hyperphosphorylation in late G1 phase. Complexes of the three D-type cyclins with CDK4 reconstituted in insect (Sf9) cells phosphorylate a pRb fusion protein at the same sites phosphorylated in human T cells. Furthermore, coexpression of cyclin D-CDK4 complexes induces pRb phosphorylation and prevents the interaction between the transcription factor E2F-1 and pRb, thereby relieving E2F repression.

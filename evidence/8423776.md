# Analysis of the SWI4/SWI6 protein complex, which directs G1/S-specific transcription in Saccharomyces cerevisiae

- **PMID**: 8423776
- **Authors**: J. Sidorova, L. Breeden
- **Journal**: Molecular and Cellular Biology (1993) 13:1069-1077
- **DOI**: 10.1128/mcb.13.2.1069-1077.1993

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-005` (`SWI4` → `SWI6`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The transcription factor SBF, which directs Start-specific transcription in budding yeast, is a heteromeric complex composed of Swi4 and Swi6 that binds specifically to SCB (SWI4/6-dependent cell cycle box) sequences in the promoters of the HO and G1 cyclin genes. In this study, domain analysis of Swi4 and Swi6 revealed that while Swi4 contains the sequence-specific DNA-binding domain, the C-terminal region of Swi4 is necessary and sufficient for association with Swi6. Furthermore, although the ankyrin repeats (TPLH repeats) and leucine zipper region of Swi6 are not required for complex formation with Swi4 per se, they are required for the Swi4/Swi6 complex to bind DNA at SCB sequences, leading to a model in which Swi6 regulates the exposure and accessibility of the Swi4 DNA-binding domain.

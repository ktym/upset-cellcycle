# Conserved homeodomain proteins interact with MADS box protein Mcm1 to restrict ECB-dependent transcription to the M/G1 phase of the cell cycle

- **PMID**: 12464633
- **Authors**: Pramila T, Miles S, GuhaThakurta D, Jemiolo D, Breeden LL
- **Journal**: Genes Dev (2002) 16:3034-3045
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.1034302
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12464633/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-061` (`YOX1|YHP1` → `MCM1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

At the M/G1 boundary in budding yeast, transient transcription of genes essential for G1/S progression and DNA replication initiation—such as *CLN3*, *SWI4*, *CDC6*, and *MCM* family genes—is induced via the Early Cell Cycle Box (ECB) element. Although the MADS-box protein Mcm1 is constitutively bound to ECB elements, how its transcriptional activity is restricted strictly to the M/G1 phase remained unclear. This study revealed that two homeodomain proteins, Yox1 and Yhp1, bind directly to Mcm1 and form a transcriptional repressor complex on ECB elements. Yox1 and Yhp1 are expressed from G1/S through M phase to bind Mcm1 on ECBs and repress transcription, thereby strictly restricting ECB-dependent transcription to the M/G1 phase.

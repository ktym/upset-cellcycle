# A Bub1-Mad1 interaction targets the Mad1-Mad2 complex to unattached kinetochores to initiate the spindle checkpoint

- **PMID**: 24567362
- **Authors**: Mark W. Moyle, Taekyung Kim, Neil Hattersley, Julien Espeut, Dhanya K. Cheerambathur, Karen Oegema, Arshad Desai
- **Journal**: Journal of Cell Biology (2014) 204:647-657
- **DOI**: 10.1083/jcb.201311015
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24567362/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N31` (`MPS1` → `BUB1`), `SCE-CC-N32` (`BUB1` → `MAD1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Initiation of the spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) requires localization of the Mad1-Mad2 complex to unattached kinetochores to catalyze generation of the APC/C-inhibitory signal. This study reveals that a direct physical interaction between Bub1 and Mad1 is a conserved mechanism targeting the Mad1-Mad2 complex to unattached kinetochores to initiate the spindle checkpoint. Together with Mps1-dependent phosphorylation-mediated Bub1-Mad1 interaction in budding yeast (London & Biggins 2014), these findings establish that kinetochore-bound Bub1 functions as a direct receptor for the Mad1-Mad2 complex.

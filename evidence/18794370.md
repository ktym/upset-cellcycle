## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates the role of the regulator Stb1 in transcriptional control mediated by the G1/S-specific transcription factor complexes SBF (Swi4-Swi6) and MBF (Mbp1-Swi6). Stb1 acts as a stable component of both complexes via their shared subunit Swi6 and binds to G1-specific gene promoters during G1 phase. In early G1, Stb1 collaborates with Sin3 (a histone deacetylase complex component) in transcriptional repression prior to Cln3-dependent activation, whereas from late G1 through the G1/S transition it also contributes to modulating the level and timing of transcriptional induction, dissociating from promoters upon phosphorylation at the end of G1 phase.

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Although p27 (CDKN1B) and p21 (CDKN1A) are classically known as cyclin-dependent kinase (CDK) inhibitors, they have also been implicated in the assembly and activation of CDK4–cyclin D complexes. Using structural and biochemical approaches, this study demonstrated that tyrosine-phosphorylated p27 (phosp27) forms a trimeric complex with CDK4–cyclin D1 and allosterically activates CDK4–cyclin D1 by remodeling the ATP-binding site of CDK4. By contrast, p21 was shown to be a poor allosteric activator. Furthermore, purified and endogenous active phosp27–CDK4–cyclin D1 trimers are insensitive to the clinically approved CDK4/6 inhibitor palbociclib, which instead primarily binds to monomeric CDK4/6.

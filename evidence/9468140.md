## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The retinoblastoma protein (Rb) binds to E2F family transcription factors and represses the transcription of G1/S target genes required for cell-cycle progression. In this study, transcriptional repression by Rb was shown to involve the recruitment of the histone deacetylase HDAC1. Rb forms a complex with HDAC1 to promote histone deacetylation at E2F target promoters, actively repressing E2F-dependent transcription by altering chromatin structure.

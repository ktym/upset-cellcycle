# Separase cooperates with Zds1 and Zds2 to activate Cdc14 phosphatase in early anaphase

- **PMID**: 18762578
- **Authors**: Ethel Queralt, Frank Uhlmann
- **Journal**: Journal of Cell Biology (2008) 182:873-883
- **DOI**: 10.1083/jcb.200801054
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18762578/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N25` (`ZDS1|ZDS2` → `CDC55`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Completion of mitotic exit and cytokinesis in budding yeast requires the elimination of mitotic Cdk activity through activation of the Cdc14 phosphatase. At anaphase onset, separase (Esp1)-dependent downregulation of PP2A-Cdc55 allows Net1 phosphorylation to proceed, releasing Cdc14 from the nucleolus. This study reveals that the Cdc55-interacting proteins Zds1 and Zds2 act downstream of separase to negatively regulate PP2A-Cdc55 activity, thereby promoting Cdc14 release in early anaphase and mitotic progression.

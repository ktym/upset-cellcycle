# Recruitment of Thr 319-phosphorylated Ndd1p to the FHA domain of Fkh2p requires Clb kinase activity: a mechanism for CLB cluster gene activation

- **PMID**: 12865300
- **Authors**: David Reynolds, Bu Jun Shi, Cameron McLean, Frosa Katsis, Bruce Kemp, Stephen Dalton
- **Journal**: Genes & Development (2003) 17:1789-1802
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.1074103
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12865300/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-028` (`CDC28|CLB2` → `NDD1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Transcriptional activation of the *CLB2* gene cluster during G2/M phase in budding yeast requires the cell-cycle-dependent recruitment of the coactivator Ndd1p to the Mcm1-Fkh2 complex bound to promoters. This study revealed that this recruitment is mediated by direct binding between the Forkhead-associated (FHA) phosphopeptide recognition domain of Fkh2p and phosphorylated Ndd1p. Furthermore, it demonstrated that direct phosphorylation of the Thr-319 residue of Ndd1p by Clb2-Cdc28 (Clb-CDK) kinase is essential for binding to the Fkh2p FHA domain and recruitment to promoters, elucidating a positive feedback mechanism in which Clb2-Cdc28 phosphorylates Ndd1p to further promote its own transcription (the *CLB2* cluster).

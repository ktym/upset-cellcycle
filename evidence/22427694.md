# Zds1 regulates PP2A(Cdc55) activity and Cdc14 activation during mitotic exit through its Zds_C motif

- **PMID**: 22427694
- **Authors**: Inés Calabria, Bàrbara Baro, José-Antonio Rodríguez-Rodríguez, Núria Russiñol, Ethel Queralt
- **Journal**: Journal of Cell Science (2012) 125:2875-2884
- **DOI**: 10.1242/jcs.097865
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22427694/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N25` (`ZDS1|ZDS2` → `CDC55`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

At anaphase onset in budding yeast, separase and Zds1 cooperate to downregulate PP2A-Cdc55 phosphatase activity, inducing Net1 phosphorylation and Cdc14 release from the nucleolus. This study reveals that a conserved Zds_C motif in the C-terminus of Zds1 is required for its physical interaction with Cdc55 and for controlling the subcellular localization of Cdc55. By directly binding to Cdc55 and regulating both its localization and phosphatase activity, Zds1 supports proper mitotic progression and exit.

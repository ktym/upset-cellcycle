# DNA damage-induced activation of p53 by the checkpoint kinase Chk2

- **PMID**: 10710310
- **Authors**: A. Hirao, Y. Y. Kong, S. Matsuoka, A. Wakeham, J. Ruland, H. Yoshida, D. Liu, S. J. Elledge, T. W. Mak
- **Journal**: Science (2000) 287:1824-1827
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.287.5459.1824
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10710310/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-012` (`CHEK2` → `TP53`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Generation and analysis of mouse cells deficient in the checkpoint kinase Chk2 (CHEK2) (Chk2-/-) revealed that p53 stabilization and transcriptional induction of p53 target genes such as p21 following gamma-irradiation are impaired, leading to defects in cell cycle checkpoint arrest and apoptosis induction. Furthermore, activated Chk2 directly phosphorylates p53 on Ser-20 to block Mdm2-mediated p53 degradation, thereby directly contributing to p53 stabilization and activation in response to DNA damage.

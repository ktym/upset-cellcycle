# Hog1 mediates cell-cycle arrest in G1 phase by the dual targeting of Sic1

- **PMID**: 15448699
- **Authors**: Xavier Escoté, Meritxell Zapater, Joaquim Clotet, Francesc Posas
- **Journal**: Nature Cell Biology (2004) 6:997-1002
- **DOI**: 10.1038/ncb1174

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N08` (`HOG1` → `SIC1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates the molecular mechanism by which the stress-activated MAP kinase Hog1, activated in response to hyperosmotic stress, arrests the budding yeast cell cycle in G1 phase. Hog1 not only downregulates G1 cyclin (CLN1/CLN2) expression, but also directly binds to the CDK inhibitor Sic1 and phosphorylates a single residue near its C-terminus, Thr173 (T173). This Hog1-mediated phosphorylation of Sic1 on T173 interferes with its interaction with and ubiquitination by the SCF(Cdc4) ubiquitin ligase complex, thereby stabilizing and accumulating Sic1 to inhibit entry into S phase and arrest cells in G1.

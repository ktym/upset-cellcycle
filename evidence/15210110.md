# Cln3 activates G1-specific transcription via phosphorylation of the SBF bound repressor Whi5

- **PMID**: 15210110
- **Authors**: Robertus A. M. de Bruin, W. Hayes McDonald, Tatyana I. Kalashnikova, John Yates 3rd, Curt Wittenberg
- **Journal**: Cell (2004) 117:887-898
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cell.2004.05.025
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15210110/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-002` (`CLN3|CDC28` → `WHI5`), `SCE-CC-003` (`WHI5` → `SWI4|SWI6`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Proteomic analysis of the SBF and MBF transcription factor complexes by mass spectrometry (MudPIT) identified Whi5 as a novel factor stably bound to SBF. Whi5 binds to G1-specific promoters via SBF during early G1 phase and functions as a transcriptional repressor that suppresses premature expression of G1-specific genes and budding. Inactivation of *WHI5* bypasses the requirement for Cln3 in transcriptional activation and cell-cycle initiation. During late G1 phase, direct phosphorylation of Whi5 by Cln3/CDK (at least five CDK phosphorylation sites in vivo) is promoted, causing Whi5 to dissociate from SBF complexes and promoters and thereby activating SBF-dependent transcription.

## Extracted Evidence

### `SCE-CC-002`: `CLN3|CDC28` → `WHI5` (`phosphorylation/inhibition`)
- **TSV Description**: Cln3-Cdc28 phosphorylates Whi5, driving its dissociation from SBF/MBF and its nuclear export.
- **Location**: Title / Abstract
- **Extracted Phrases/Sentences**:
  > "Dissociation of Whi5 is promoted by Cln3 in vivo. Cln/CDK phosphorylation of Whi5 in vitro promotes its dissociation from SBF complexes. Mutation of putative CDK phosphorylation sites, at least five of which are phosphorylated in vivo, strongly reduces SBF-dependent transcription and delays cell cycle initiation."
- **Evidence Explanation**: This demonstrates that Cln3-Cdc28 (Cln/CDK) directly phosphorylates Whi5 and promotes its dissociation from SBF complexes, relieving Whi5-mediated transcriptional repression and inducing cell-cycle initiation.

### `SCE-CC-003`: `WHI5` → `SWI4|SWI6` (`inhibition (binding)`)
- **TSV Description**: Whi5 binds SBF at promoters and represses G1/S transcription.
- **Location**: Abstract
- **Extracted Phrases/Sentences**:
  > "Whi5 was identified as a stably bound component of SBF... Whi5 associates with G1-specific promoters via SBF during early G1 phase, then dissociates coincident with transcriptional activation... Like mammalian Rb, Whi5 is a G1-specific transcriptional repressor antagonized by CDK."
- **Evidence Explanation**: This demonstrates that Whi5 is a stable binding component of SBF (Swi4-Swi6) that associates with promoters during early G1 phase and acts as a repressor of G1-specific transcription.

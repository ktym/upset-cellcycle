# Phosphate-regulated inactivation of the kinase PHO80-PHO85 by the CDK inhibitor PHO81

- **PMID**: 7939631
- **Authors**: K. R. Schneider, R. L. Smith, E. K. O'Shea
- **Journal**: Science (1994) 266:122-126
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.7939631
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7939631/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-016` (`PHO81` → `PHO85|PHO80`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Under high-phosphate growth conditions, the complex of the cyclin Pho80 and the CDK Pho85 phosphorylates and inactivates the transcription factor Pho4. Conversely, under low-phosphate (phosphate-depleted) growth conditions, the CDK inhibitor (CKI) Pho81 binds to the Pho80-Pho85 complex and inhibits its kinase activity, thereby preventing Pho4 phosphorylation and inducing PHO5 transcription. Furthermore, it was shown that an ankyrin repeat region within the Pho81 protein, homologous to the mammalian CDK inhibitor p16(INK4), is sufficient to inhibit Pho80-Pho85 kinase activity in vitro.

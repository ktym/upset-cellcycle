# Degradation of the SCF component Skp2 in cell-cycle phase G1 by the anaphase-promoting complex

- **PMID**: 15014503
- **Authors**: Wenyi Wei, Nagi G. Ayad, Yong Wan, Guo-Jun Zhang, Marc W. Kirschner, William G. Kaelin Jr.
- **Journal**: Nature (2004) 428:194-198
- **DOI**: 10.1038/nature02381
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15014503/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-053` (`FZR1` → `SKP2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Cell-cycle transitions are driven by the degradation of key regulatory factors by two major E3 ubiquitin ligases, the SCF complex and the anaphase-promoting complex (APC/C), and the primary target of the SCF-Skp2 complex is the CDK inhibitor p27 (CDKN1B). This study revealed that the F-box protein Skp2 (SKP2) itself is polyubiquitinated in G1 phase by the Cdh1 (FZR1)-containing APC complex (APC-CDH1) and targeted for proteasomal degradation. The N-terminal region of Skp2 contains a D-box motif recognized by APC-CDH1, demonstrating that because of this degradation mechanism, accumulation of the SCF-Skp2 complex and initiation of p27 degradation for S-phase progression require prior inactivation of APC-CDH1.

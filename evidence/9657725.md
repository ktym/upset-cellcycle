# Rad53 FHA domain associated with phosphorylated Rad9 in the DNA damage checkpoint

- **PMID**: 9657725
- **Authors**: Sun Z, Hsiao J, Fay DS, Stern DF
- **Journal**: Science (1998) 281:272-274
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.281.5374.272
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9657725/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-045` (`RAD9` → `RAD53`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The budding yeast Rad53 protein kinase is required for checkpoints that prevent cell division in cells with damaged or incompletely replicated DNA. This study revealed that the checkpoint protein Rad9 is phosphorylated in response to DNA damage and that phosphorylated Rad9 interacts with the C-terminal forkhead homology-associated (FHA) domain (FHA2) of Rad53. Inactivation of this FHA domain by mutation abolished DNA damage-dependent Rad53 phosphorylation and G2/M cell cycle arrest. These findings demonstrate that direct binding between phosphorylated Rad9 and the Rad53 FHA domain is essential for DNA damage signaling and Rad53 activation.

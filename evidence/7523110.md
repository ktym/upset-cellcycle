# Activation of the phosphatase activity of human cdc25A by a cdk2-cyclin E dependent phosphorylation at the G1/S transition

- **PMID**: 7523110
- **Authors**: I. Hoffmann, G. Draetta, E. Karsenti
- **Journal**: The EMBO Journal (1994) 13:4302-4310
- **DOI**: 10.1002/j.1460-2075.1994.tb06750.x
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7523110/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-019` (`CDC25A` → `CDK2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The human cdc25 family phosphatase cdc25A (CDC25A) undergoes phosphorylation from the G1/S transition into S phase, resulting in an increase in its phosphatase activity. This phosphorylation and activation of cdc25A depend on Cdk2-cyclin E kinase activity, and microinjection of anti-cdc25A antibodies into G1 cells blocks entry into S phase. Therefore, cdc25A is an essential phosphatase at the G1/S transition that removes inhibitory phosphorylation from CDK2 to activate it, forming a positive feedback (auto-amplification) loop with cyclin E-CDK2 to drive S-phase entry.

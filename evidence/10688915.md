## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The oncogene c-MYC is a transcription factor that drives cell proliferation by promoting cell cycle re-entry. Using serial analysis of gene expression (SAGE), this study identified the cyclin-dependent kinase 4 (CDK4) gene as a direct transcriptional target of c-MYC. c-MYC rapidly induces CDK4 mRNA levels through four highly conserved c-MYC binding sites (E-boxes) within the CDK4 promoter, directly linking the growth-promoting activity of c-MYC to G1 cell cycle progression.

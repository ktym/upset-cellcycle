# The Polo-like kinase Cdc5 interacts with FEAR network components and Cdc14

- **PMID**: 18927509
- **Authors**: Rahal R, Amon A
- **Journal**: Cell Cycle (2008) 7:3262-3272
- **DOI**: 10.4161/cc.7.20.6852
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18927509/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-060` (`CDC5` → `CDC14|ESP1|SLK19`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In budding yeast, the phosphatase Cdc14 is essential for mitotic exit, and its release from the nucleolus is regulated by the FEAR (Cdc Fourteen Early Anaphase Release) network during early anaphase and by the Mitotic Exit Network (MEN) during late anaphase. The Polo-like kinase Cdc5 plays a central role in Cdc14 release as a component of both the FEAR network and the MEN, but its molecular mechanism within the FEAR network had not been fully understood. This study demonstrates that Cdc5 physically associates with the FEAR network components separase (Esp1) and the kinetochore-associated protein Slk19, and also directly interacts with Cdc14. These interactions require the conserved Polo-box domain (a phosphoserine/phosphothreonine-binding domain) of Cdc5, showing that complex formation mediated by the Polo-box domain is essential for promoting Cdc14 release during early anaphase.

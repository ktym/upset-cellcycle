# The budding yeast protein kinase Ipl1/Aurora allows the absence of tension to activate the spindle checkpoint

- **PMID**: 11731476
- **Authors**: Sue Biggins, Andrew W. Murray
- **Journal**: Genes & Development (2001) 15:3118-3129
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.934801
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11731476/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N33` (`IPL1|SLI15` → `MAD2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) delays anaphase onset when abnormalities exist in the mitotic spindle, but how unattached kinetochores and the absence of tension are distinguished and transduced remained unclear. This study demonstrated that Ipl1, the budding yeast Aurora B kinase, is dispensable for checkpoint activation upon microtubule depolymerization (unattached kinetochores), but is essential for activating and maintaining the spindle checkpoint in the absence of tension between sister chromatids (such as in cells entering mitosis without DNA replication or in cohesin mutants). Ipl1 destabilizes tensionless kinetochore-microtubule attachments to generate unattached kinetochores, thereby indirectly switching on SAC pathways involving Mad2.

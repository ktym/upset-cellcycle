- **Journal**: Cell (2003) 112:697-709

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

During anaphase in budding yeast, the Ras-related GTPase Tem1 activates the mitotic exit network (MEN) on the spindle pole body (SPB) entering the daughter cell, inducing exit from mitosis. However, in G1 phase after mitotic exit is complete, MEN activity must be rapidly extinguished to reset the cell cycle. This study revealed that the daughter-cell-specifically induced Amn1 protein binds directly to Tem1 and prevents the association between Tem1 and its target kinase Cdc15, thereby shutting off MEN signaling and driving Cdc14 inactivation and cell-cycle resetting in G1 phase.

# Human F-box protein hCdc4 targets cyclin E for proteolysis and is mutated in a breast cancer cell line

- **PMID**: 11565533
- **Authors**: Heimo Strohmaier, Charles H. Spruck, Peter Kaiser, Kwang-Ai Won, Olle Sangfelt, Steven I. Reed
- **Journal**: Nature (2001) 413:316-322
- **DOI**: 10.1038/35095076
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11565533/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-029` (`FBXW7` → `CCNE1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Cyclin E (CCNE1), an activator of Cdk2, is expressed during the G1/S transition and is essential for the initiation of DNA replication, but it must be rapidly eliminated via autophosphorylation-coupled proteolysis as S phase progresses. This study revealed that the turnover of phosphorylated cyclin E depends on an SCF-type ubiquitin ligase complex containing the WD40-repeat F-box protein hCdc4 (FBXW7), the human homologue of budding yeast Cdc4. SCF-hCdc4 specifically binds phosphorylated cyclin E and directs it toward ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation. Furthermore, mutations in the *hCdc4* gene were identified in a human breast cancer cell line exhibiting abnormally high cyclin E expression, indicating that disruption of SCF-FBXW7-mediated cyclin E degradation contributes to genomic instability and tumorigenesis.

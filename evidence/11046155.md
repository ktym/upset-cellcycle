# Chromatin association of human origin recognition complex, cdc6, and minichromosome maintenance proteins during the cell cycle: assembly of prereplication complexes in late mitosis

- **PMID**: 11046155
- **Authors**: J. Méndez, B. Stillman
- **Journal**: Molecular and Cellular Biology (2000) 20:8602-8612
- **DOI**: 10.1128/MCB.20.22.8602-8612.2000

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Initiation of DNA replication requires the assembly of prereplication complexes (pre-RCs) (licensing) by the origin recognition complex (ORC), Cdc6, and minichromosome maintenance (MCM) proteins. In this study, chromatin fractionation analysis in human cells revealed that while human ORC remains bound to chromatin throughout the cell cycle, hCdc6p and hMcm proteins (MCM2–7) cooperatively associate with chromatin from late mitosis to early G1 phase to assemble pre-RCs.

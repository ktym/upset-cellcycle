# Mrc1 transduces signals of DNA replication stress to activate Rad53

- **PMID**: 11715016

- **Journal**: Nat Cell Biol (2001) 3:958-965
- **DOI**: 10.1038/ncb1101-958
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11715016/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-046` (`MRC1` → `RAD53`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The S-phase checkpoint responding to DNA replication stress suppresses late origin firing and stabilizes replication forks upon fork stalling to maintain genomic integrity. This study identified Mrc1 (Mediator of the Replication Checkpoint) as a novel mediator (adaptor) factor that transduces replication stress signals in budding yeast and fission yeast. In budding yeast, Mrc1 participates in normal DNA replication fork progression and is phosphorylated upon replication stress such as hydroxyurea (HU) treatment, where it is specifically required for the activation of the effector kinase Rad53. Unlike the DNA damage adaptor Rad9, Mrc1 functions as an adaptor specialized for the S-phase replication checkpoint, mediating the activation signal from Mec1 to Rad53.

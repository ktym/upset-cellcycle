# Mechanisms of pseudosubstrate inhibition of the anaphase promoting complex by Acm1

- **PMID**: 21460798
- **Authors**: Janet L. Burton, Yong Xiong, Mark J. Solomon
- **Journal**: The EMBO Journal (2011) 30:1818-1829
- **DOI**: 10.1038/emboj.2011.90
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21460798/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N20` (`ACM1|BMH1|BMH2` → `CDH1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates the structural and biochemical mechanisms by which Acm1 inhibits Cdh1 as a pseudosubstrate without being ubiquitinated as an APC/C-Cdh1 substrate. In addition to its central D-box (destruction box) and KEN-box, Acm1 uses an A-motif to bind extremely tightly to the WD40 domain of Cdh1, competitively blocking authentic substrates from binding to Cdh1. Furthermore, potentially ubiquitinatable lysine residues within Acm1 are positioned in a sterically inaccessible configuration, demonstrating that the combination of high-affinity Cdh1 binding and lysine inaccessibility enables Acm1 to function as a pseudosubstrate inhibitor.

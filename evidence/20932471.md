# CRL4(Cdt2) regulates cell proliferation and histone gene expression by targeting PR-Set7/Set8 for degradation

- **PMID**: 20932471
- **Authors**: Tarek Abbas, Etsuko Shibata, Jonghoon Park, Sudhakar Jha, Neerja Karnani, Anindya Dutta
- **Journal**: Molecular Cell (2010) 40:9-21
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.molcel.2010.09.014
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20932471/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N14` (`DTL|DDB1|CUL4A|PCNA` → `CDT1|CDKN1A|KMT5A`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The histone H4 lysine 20 monomethyltransferase PR-Set7/Set8 (KMT5A) is an essential enzyme for cell-cycle progression and chromatin structure regulation that is rapidly degraded during S phase. This study reveals that the CRL4(Cdt2) (CUL4-DDB1-DTL) E3 ubiquitin ligase complex recognizes PCNA-bound PR-Set7/Set8 on chromatin during S phase and after UV irradiation, targeting it for ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation. Binding to PCNA via the PIP box of Set8 is required for this degradation, and expressing mutant Set8 resistant to PCNA binding or CRL4(Cdt2)-mediated degradation induces DNA re-replication, aberrant chromatin compaction, DNA damage, and G2 arrest.

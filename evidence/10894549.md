# Forkhead-like transcription factors recruit Ndd1 to the chromatin of G2/M-specific promoters

- **PMID**: 10894549
- **Authors**: M. Koranda, A. Schleiffer, L. Endler, G. Ammerer
- **Journal**: Nature (2000) 406:94-98
- **DOI**: 10.1038/35017589
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10894549/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-027` (`MCM1|FKH2` → `NDD1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Periodic transcriptional activation of G2/M-specific genes in budding yeast (the CLB2 cluster: CLB1, CLB2, CDC5, SWI5, etc.) involves the coordinated action of the MADS-box protein Mcm1, the forkhead transcription factors Fkh1/Fkh2, and the transcriptional coactivator Ndd1. Using chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP) assays, this study revealed that Mcm1 and Fkh2 bind to DNA on G2/M promoters as a permanent complex throughout the cell cycle, and that Ndd1, an essential positive regulator in G2/M, is periodically recruited to the promoter regions in a manner dependent on the Mcm1–Fkh2 complex. Furthermore, because the lethality of NDD1 deletion is suppressed by fkh2 deletion, Fkh2 acts as a switch that represses transcription when CDK activity is low and activates transcription by recruiting Ndd1 during G2/M.

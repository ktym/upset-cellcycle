# Functional inactivation of the retinoblastoma protein requires sequential modification by at least two distinct cyclin-cdk complexes

- **PMID**: 9447971
- **Authors**: A. S. Lundberg, R. A. Weinberg
- **Journal**: Molecular and Cellular Biology (1998) 18:753-761
- **DOI**: 10.1128/MCB.18.2.753
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9447971/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-018` (`CCNE1|CDK2` → `RB1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The retinoblastoma protein (pRb) restricts the G1/S transition and is inactivated by phosphorylation during G1 phase. In this study, experiments selectively inhibiting either CDK4/6 or CDK2 revealed that endogenous cyclin D-CDK4/6 complexes can only partially phosphorylate pRb, and that complete hyperphosphorylation of pRb requires the subsequent action of the cyclin E-CDK2 complex. Complete hyperphosphorylation of pRb, inactivation of E2F binding, and activation of E2F-mediated transcription are achieved only after sequential phosphorylation by cyclin D-CDK4/6 and cyclin E-CDK2.

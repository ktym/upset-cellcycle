# Bub3 reads phosphorylated MELT repeats to promote spindle assembly checkpoint signaling

- **PMID**: 24066227
- **Authors**: Ivana Primorac, John R. Weir, Elena Chiroli, Fridolin Gross, Ingrid Hoffmann, Suzan van Gerwen, Andrea Ciliberto, Andrea Musacchio
- **Journal**: eLife (2013) 2:e01030
- **DOI**: 10.7554/eLife.01030
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24066227/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N30` (`SPC105` → `BUB3|BUB1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In the spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC), Mps1 kinase phosphorylates multiple MELT (Met-Glu-Leu-Thr) motifs on the kinetochore protein Spc105/Knl1 to promote recruitment of downstream SAC components. Through structural and cell biological analyses, this study reveals that Bub3, which folds into a seven-bladed beta-propeller, is the direct reader (recognition and binding factor) of phosphorylated MELT motifs (MELT(P)). Introducing mutations into the conserved binding interface of Bub3 abolishes kinetochore localization of the SAC kinase Bub1 (the Bub3-Bub1 complex) and abrogates SAC signaling, demonstrating that direct recognition of phosphorylated Spc105 by Bub3 is essential for checkpoint activation.

- **Journal**: The EMBO Journal (2005) 24:2194-2204

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In budding yeast, Swe1, a kinase that inhibits mitotic entry, is recruited to the bud neck via Hsl1 and the adaptor Hsl7 and undergoes hyperphosphorylation prior to ubiquitin-dependent degradation. This study demonstrates that direct phosphorylation of Swe1 by Clb2-Cdc28 (Cdk1) maintains Swe1 catalytic activity while also serving as a priming phosphorylation that creates a docking site for the Polo-box domain (PBD) of the Polo-like kinase Cdc5. Cdc5 binds to Clb2-Cdc28-primed Swe1 and hyperphosphorylates Swe1 at the bud neck in an Hsl1/Hsl7-dependent manner, triggering rapid Swe1 degradation and mitotic entry.

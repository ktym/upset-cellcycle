# Phosphorylation by Cdc28 activates the Cdc20-dependent activity of the anaphase-promoting complex

- **PMID**: 10871279
- **Authors**: Rudner AD, Murray AW
- **Journal**: J Cell Biol (2000) 149:1377-1390
- **DOI**: 10.1083/jcb.149.7.1377
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10871279/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-041` (`CDC28` → `CDC16|CDC27|CDC23`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Budding yeast initiates anaphase by activating the Cdc20-dependent anaphase-promoting complex (APC/C). This study showed that mitotic Cdc28 (Cdk1) activity is required for activation of APC–Cdc20, and that APC phosphorylation depends on mitotic Cdc28 activity in vivo and is catalyzed by purified Cdc28 in vitro. Mutating Cdc28 consensus phosphorylation sites in three APC subunits (Cdc16, Cdc23, and Cdc27) abolished APC phosphorylation both in vivo and in vitro. Although this non-phosphorylatable APC mutant had normal G1 (Cdh1-dependent) activity, its mitotic Cdc20-dependent activity was compromised, revealing that phosphorylation of APC subunits by Cdc28 activates APC–Cdc20 to drive anaphase onset.

# CRL4(Cdt2)-mediated destruction of the histone methyltransferase Set8 prevents premature chromatin compaction in S phase

- **PMID**: 20932472
- **Authors**: Richard C. Centore, Courtney G. Havens, Amity L. Manning, Ju-Mei Li, Rachel Litman Flynn, Alice Tse, Jianping Jin, Nicholas J. Dyson, Johannes C. Walter, Lee Zou
- **Journal**: Molecular Cell (2010) 40:22-33
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.molcel.2010.09.015
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20932472/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N14` (`DTL|DDB1|CUL4A|PCNA` → `CDT1|CDKN1A|KMT5A`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The histone methyltransferase Set8 (PR-Set7 / KMT5A), responsible for histone H4K20 monomethylation, accumulates in G2/M phase to regulate chromosome condensation and cell-cycle progression, but is kept at low levels during S phase. This study demonstrates that Set8 interacts with chromatin-bound PCNA via a PIP degron and is ubiquitinated and degraded by the CRL4(Cdt2) ubiquitin ligase complex during S phase and upon DNA damage. Inhibiting CRL4(Cdt2)-mediated Set8 degradation results in aberrant persistence of H4K20me1 and premature chromatin compaction in S phase, as well as defects in DNA replication licensing and checkpoint activation, showing that PCNA-dependent substrate destruction by CRL4(Cdt2) is essential for proper chromosome replication in S phase.

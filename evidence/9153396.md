# Regulation of p53 stability by Mdm2

- **PMID**: 9153396
- **Authors**: M. H. Kubbutat, S. N. Jones, K. H. Vousden
- **Journal**: Nature (1997) 387:299-303
- **DOI**: 10.1038/387299a0
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9153396/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-013` (`MDM2` → `TP53`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The activity of the tumor suppressor protein p53 is critical for inducing cell-cycle arrest and apoptosis, and in normal cells it is tightly regulated at the level of protein stability (half-life). In this study, Mdm2 was shown to bind directly to p53 to induce its rapid proteasome-dependent degradation, maintaining p53 protein at low levels. Mutant p53 lacking the ability to bind Mdm2 is resistant to Mdm2-mediated degradation and becomes stabilized, demonstrating that Mdm2-promoted degradation is a major mechanism controlling p53 abundance.

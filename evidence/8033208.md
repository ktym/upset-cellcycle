# Negative regulation of the growth-promoting transcription factor E2F-1 by a stably bound cyclin A-dependent protein kinase

- **PMID**: 8033208

- **Journal**: Cell (1994) 78:161-172
- **DOI**: 10.1016/0092-8674(94)90582-7

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-025` (`CCNA2|CDK2` → `E2F1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Cyclin A-dependent kinase (cyclin A-CDK2), which coordinates S-phase progression, forms stable in vivo complexes with the transcription factor E2F-1, which activates gene transcription at the G1/S transition. Cyclin A-CDK2 phosphorylates the E2F-1-DP-1 heterodimer and shuts off E2F-1-dependent DNA-binding activity during S and G2 phases. This ensures that transcription of E2F target genes activated at the G1/S transition is properly terminated during S phase, guaranteeing orderly cell cycle progression.

- **Journal**: Nature (2015) 519:431-435

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This landmark study achieved the complete in vitro reconstitution of regulated eukaryotic DNA replication initiation using 16 purified Saccharomyces cerevisiae replication factors (42 polypeptides). Acting on Mcm2-7 double hexamers loaded onto DNA by ORC, Cdc6, and Cdt1, Dbf4-dependent kinase (DDK: Cdc7-Dbf4) phosphorylates Mcm4 and Mcm6 to promote recruitment of Sld3/7 and Cdc45, after which S-CDK (Clb5-Cdc28) phosphorylates Sld2 and Sld3 to assemble Dpb11, GINS, and Pol epsilon into the active CMG helicase. This reconstituted system definitively established that Mcm4 and Mcm6 are the essential targets of DDK in replication initiation.

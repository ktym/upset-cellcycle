- **Journal**: Cell (2016) 166:167-180
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cell.2016.05.077

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-022` (`FBXO5` → `FZR1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Using single-cell live imaging, this study investigated the molecular identity of the "point of no return" at which mammalian cells progress from G1 into S phase and irreversibly commit to the cell cycle. Rapid and irreversible inactivation of the ubiquitin ligase complex APC/C-Cdh1 (FZR1) at the G1/S transition was found to be the true point of no return beyond which cells can no longer revert to G0/G1 even under cellular stress. This irreversible inactivation is driven by the cooperative action of cyclin E/CDK2 and the APC/C-Cdh1 inhibitor protein Emi1 (FBXO5), forming a bistable switch that determines commitment to S-phase entry.

- **Journal**: Journal of Cell Biology (2007) 179:423-436
- **DOI**: 10.1083/jcb.200705197

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The spindle position checkpoint (SPOC) in budding yeast delays mitotic exit by maintaining the activity of the Bfa1–Bub2 GAP complex when the anaphase spindle becomes misaligned due to cytoplasmic microtubule defects. This study reveals that Spc72, a component of the spindle pole body (SPB, the yeast centrosome equivalent), recruits the Kin4 kinase to both SPBs in response to cytoplasmic microtubule defects. At the SPB, Kin4 directly phosphorylates Bfa1, preventing inhibitory phosphorylation of Bfa1 by the Polo-like kinase Cdc5 and thereby keeping Bfa1–Bub2 active to block mitotic exit.

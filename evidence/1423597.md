## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Mammalian D-type G1 cyclins (cyclins D1, D2, and D3) bind and form complexes with p34PSK-J3 (CDK4), an atypical catalytic subunit distinct from previously known CDKs (cdc2, cdk2, and cdk3). In macrophages, cyclin D1-CDK4 complexes accumulate during G1 phase and decline as cells enter S phase. While reconstituted cyclin D-CDK4 complexes lack histone H1 kinase activity, they stably bind and phosphorylate the retinoblastoma protein (pRb) and p107, functioning as a pRb-regulating kinase during G1 progression.

# Homeostatic control of START through negative feedback between Cln3-Cdk1 and Rim15/Greatwall kinase in budding yeast

- **PMID**: 28594326
- **Authors**: Nicolas Talarek, Élodie Gueydon, Emmanuel Schwob
- **Journal**: eLife (2017) 6:e26233
- **DOI**: 10.7554/eLife.26233
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28594326/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N06` (`CDC55` → `WHI5`), `SCE-CC-N07` (`CLN3|CDC28` → `RIM15`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidated the role of a negative feedback loop between the Greatwall kinase ortholog pathway (Rim15–Igo1/2) and Cln3–Cdk1 (Cdc28) in regulating cell-size homeostasis and the Start transition in response to nutrient conditions in budding yeast. The PP2A-Cdc55 phosphatase dephosphorylates the Start repressor Whi5 to maintain it in its active state and delay Start; however, in early G1 or under nutrient-poor conditions, Rim15 phosphorylates Igo1/2 to inhibit PP2A-Cdc55, thereby promoting Whi5 phosphorylation and Start entry. Conversely, under nutrient-rich conditions when Cln3–Cdk1 activity is high, Cln3–Cdk1 phosphorylates and inhibits Rim15, maintaining homeostatic control of Whi5 phosphorylation levels and entry into Start at an appropriate cell size.

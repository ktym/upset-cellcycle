# Cell cycle regulation of the yeast Cdc7 protein kinase by association with the Dbf4 protein

- **PMID**: 8474449

- **Journal**: Molecular and Cellular Biology (1993) 13:2899-2908
- **DOI**: 10.1128/mcb.13.5.2899-2908.1993
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8474449/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-024` (`DBF4` → `CDC7`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Both the Cdc7 protein kinase and the Dbf4 protein of budding yeast are essential for the initiation of chromosomal DNA replication at the G1/S boundary. While Cdc7 protein levels remain constant throughout the cell cycle, Cdc7 kinase activity fluctuates periodically and peaks at the G1/S boundary. This study demonstrated that Cdc7 kinase activity is thermolabile in extracts from a temperature-sensitive dbf4 mutant, and proved through in vitro reconstitution and two-hybrid analysis that Dbf4 and Cdc7 directly interact physically. Thus, it was established that Dbf4 binds to Cdc7 as a cyclin-like regulatory subunit responsible for activating Cdc7 kinase at the G1/S boundary.

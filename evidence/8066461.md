# Direct inhibition of the yeast cyclin-dependent kinase Cdc28-Cln by Far1

- **PMID**: 8066461
- **Authors**: M. Peter, I. Herskowitz
- **Journal**: Science (1994) 265:1228-1231
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.8066461

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

G1 cell cycle arrest induced by mating pheromone (α-factor) in budding yeast is caused by inhibition of Cdc28-Cln kinase complexes via activation of the pheromone signal transduction pathway. The Far1 protein is essential for this cell cycle arrest and associates with Cdc28-Cln complexes in an α-factor-dependent manner. Through biochemical reconstitution experiments and in vivo analyses, this study proved that phosphorylated and activated Far1 is a CDK inhibitor (CKI) that directly inhibits the kinase activity of Cdc28-Cln1 and Cdc28-Cln2 complexes, establishing that Far1 directly suppresses G1-CDKs at the final step of the pheromone response pathway.

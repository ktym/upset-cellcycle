- **PMID**: 27272642

- **Journal**: PLoS Genetics (2016) 12:e1006088
- **DOI**: 10.1371/journal.pgen.1006088

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N10` (`MSA1|MSA2` → `SWI4|MBP1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study demonstrates that the paralogous transcriptional regulators Msa1 and Msa2 bind to Swi4 and Mbp1—the DNA-binding subunits of SBF (Swi4–Swi6) and MBF (Mbp1–Swi6), respectively—to control G1 arrest and the transition to quiescence upon nutrient (glucose) depletion. Msa1 and Msa2 form complexes with SBF and MBF to modulate the G1/S-specific transcriptional program, promoting G1 arrest under nutrient limitation through repression of SBF target genes and proper regulation of MBF target genes. In `msa1 msa2` double mutant cells, dysregulation of G1/S transcription impairs both G1 arrest and entry into quiescence, establishing Msa1/Msa2 as key transcriptional regulators acting on both SBF and MBF.

# Human T cell leukemia virus type 1 oncoprotein Tax targets the human mitotic checkpoint protein MAD1

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) ensures accurate chromosome segregation during mitosis, and its disruption leads to aneuploidy and tumorigenesis. This study identified and cloned human MAD1 (MAD1L1; TXBP181), the human homolog of budding yeast Mad1p, and revealed that human MAD1 forms a tight complex with the checkpoint factor MAD2 (MAD2L1) and localizes to kinetochores. Furthermore, the human T-cell leukemia virus type 1 (HTLV-1) oncoprotein Tax directly binds to the coiled-coil region of MAD1 to disrupt the function and localization of the MAD1-MAD2 complex, causing mitotic checkpoint defects and multinucleation (chromosomal instability).

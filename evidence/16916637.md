## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates how the transcription factor complex MBF (Mbp1-Swi6), which drives G1/S-specific transcription in budding yeast (and fission yeast), is inactivated upon entry into S phase. Nrm1 (Negative regulator of MBF targets 1), which is transcriptionally induced in late G1 as an MBF target gene itself, accumulates and directly binds as a corepressor to promoter-bound MBF complexes, repressing MBF-dependent transcription and shutting it off as cells enter S phase. Through this negative feedback loop between MBF and Nrm1, the expression of DNA replication-related genes (MBF target genes) is strictly confined to the appropriate time window of late G1 to early S phase.

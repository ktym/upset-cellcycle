# Protein kinase Cdc15 activates the Dbf2-Mob1 kinase complex

- **PMID**: 11404483
- **Authors**: Mah AS, Jang J, Deshaies RJ
- **Journal**: Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (2001) 98:7325-7330
- **DOI**: 10.1073/pnas.141098998
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11404483/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-054` (`CDC15` → `DBF2|MOB1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In the budding yeast mitotic exit network (MEN), the protein kinases Cdc15 and Dbf2 and the Dbf2-binding protein Mob1 are essential for releasing the phosphatase Cdc14 from the nucleolus and inactivating Cdk. Using biochemical reconstitution with a baculovirus expression system, this study revealed that Cdc15 directly phosphorylates Dbf2 and switches on its kinase activity. Activation of Dbf2 by Cdc15 strictly requires Mob1 binding, and Cdc15 directly phosphorylates Ser-374 and Thr-544 of Dbf2, which correspond to phosphorylation motifs conserved in the protein kinase A (PKA) family. This established a kinase cascade within the MEN in which Cdc15 directly phosphorylates and activates Mob1-bound Dbf2.

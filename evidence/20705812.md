# Histone H3 Thr-3 phosphorylation by Haspin positions Aurora B at centromeres in mitosis

- **PMID**: 20705812

- **Journal**: Science (2010) 330:231-235
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1189435
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20705812/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N20` (`HASPIN` → `BIRC5|AURKB|INCENP`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The chromosomal passenger complex (CPC, composed of Aurora B, INCENP, Survivin, and Borealin) concentrates at the inner centromere during mitosis to correct erroneous kinetochore–microtubule attachments and ensure accurate chromosome segregation. This study reveals that the mitotic kinase Haspin phosphorylates histone H3 at threonine 3 (H3T3), and that the CPC subunit Survivin (BIRC5) binds directly to the resulting phosphorylated histone H3 (H3T3ph). This Haspin-mediated H3T3 phosphorylation and its recognition by Survivin are shown to be essential for properly positioning and activating the CPC (Aurora B) at the inner centromere of mitotic chromosomes.

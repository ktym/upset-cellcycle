# Regulation of PHO4 nuclear localization by the PHO80-PHO85 cyclin-CDK complex

- **PMID**: 8539622
- **Authors**: Elizabeth M. O'Neill, Alexander Kaffman, Erin R. Jolly, Erin K. O'Shea
- **Journal**: Science (1996) 271:209-212
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.271.5246.209
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8539622/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-015` (`PHO85|PHO80` → `PHO4`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The transcription factor Pho4, required for transcriptional induction of the phosphate-starvation response gene PHO5, is phosphorylated by the Pho80-Pho85 cyclin-CDK complex in cells grown in high-phosphate medium. In this study, Pho4 was found to be concentrated in the nucleus during phosphate starvation, whereas it localized predominantly to the cytoplasm in phosphate-rich medium. Five serine-proline (SP) motif phosphorylation sites on Pho4 targeted by Pho80-Pho85 were identified, and phosphorylation of these sites was proven to be required for cytoplasmic localization of Pho4 and full repression of PHO5 transcription under high-phosphate conditions.

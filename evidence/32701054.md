# Antagonistic control of DDK binding to licensed replication origins by Mcm2 and Rad53

- **PMID**: 32701054
- **Authors**: Safuan Abd Wahab, Dirk Remus
- **Journal**: eLife (2020) 9:e58571
- **DOI**: 10.7554/eLife.58571
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32701054/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N17` (`RAD53` → `CDC7|DBF4`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Using a reconstituted budding yeast DNA replication system with purified proteins, this study elucidated how the binding of Dbf4-dependent kinase (DDK: Cdc7–Dbf4) to licensed replication origins (Mcm2–7 double hexamers) is antagonistically controlled by Mcm2 and the checkpoint kinase Rad53. Under unperturbed conditions, the flexible N-terminal extension (NTE) of Mcm2 promotes the stable recruitment of DDK to Mcm2–7 double hexamers, driving phosphorylation of Mcm4 and Mcm6 and subsequent replication origin firing. Conversely, upon S-phase checkpoint activation, activated Rad53 inhibits DDK binding to Mcm2–7 double hexamers in a kinase-activity-independent (steric) manner, thereby suppressing replication initiation.

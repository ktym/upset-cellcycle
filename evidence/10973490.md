# Ataxia telangiectasia-mutated phosphorylates Chk2 in vivo and in vitro

- **PMID**: 10973490

- **Journal**: Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America (2000) 97:10389-10394
- **DOI**: 10.1073/pnas.190030497
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10973490/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-010` (`ATM` → `CHEK2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The checkpoint kinase Chk2 (CHEK2) is phosphorylated and activated in an ATM-dependent manner in response to DNA damage caused by ionizing radiation (IR). This study demonstrated that ATM directly phosphorylates the SQ/TQ cluster domain (SCD) of Chk2 in vitro and in vivo, and that Thr-68 (T68) is the major phosphorylation site. A mutant in which Thr-68 was substituted with alanine showed markedly reduced phosphorylation and activation of Chk2 following IR exposure, demonstrating that direct phosphorylation of Chk2 on T68 by ATM in response to DNA double-strand breaks is essential for Chk2 activation.

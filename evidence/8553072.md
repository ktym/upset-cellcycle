## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Cell-cycle checkpoints responding to DNA damage and DNA replication blocks are essential for maintaining genomic integrity in eukaryotes. In this study, the essential budding yeast protein kinase Rad53p was shown to be phosphorylated and enzymatically activated in response to DNA damage and DNA replication inhibition. This phosphorylation of Rad53p depends on the ATM-like homologs Mec1p and Tel1p, and the loss of phosphorylation in a MEC1 mutant was shown to be suppressed by overexpression of TEL1. This established a checkpoint kinase cascade in which Mec1 and Tel1 act upstream of Rad53 to regulate its phosphorylation and activation.

# MAD3 encodes a novel component of the spindle checkpoint which interacts with Bub3p, Cdc20p, and Mad2p

- **PMID**: 10704439
- **Authors**: Hardwick KG, Johnston RC, Smith DL, Murray AW
- **Journal**: J Cell Biol (2000) 148:871-882
- **DOI**: 10.1083/jcb.148.5.871
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10704439/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-036` (`MAD3` → `BUB3|CDC20|MAD2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study revealed that MAD3, a component of the budding yeast spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC), encodes a novel 58-kDa nuclear protein. Mad3p contains two regions of high homology (46% and 47% identity) to the N-terminal region of the Bub1 protein kinase. Two-hybrid assays and co-immunoprecipitation experiments showed that Mad3p not only binds to Bub3p, but also physically interacts with Mad2p and the checkpoint target and cell cycle regulator Cdc20p. The regions of homology to Bub1p are essential for these intermolecular interactions, and mutations in them cause loss of checkpoint function.

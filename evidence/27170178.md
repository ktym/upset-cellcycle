# Dual mechanisms regulate the recruitment of spindle assembly checkpoint proteins to the budding yeast kinetochore

- **PMID**: 27170178
- **Authors**: P. Aravamudhan, R. Chen, B. Roy, J. Sim, Ajit P. Joglekar
- **Journal**: Molecular Biology of the Cell (2016) 27:3405-3417
- **DOI**: 10.1091/mbc.E16-01-0007
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27170178/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N31` (`MPS1` → `BUB1`), `SCE-CC-N32` (`BUB1` → `MAD1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) activation at unattached kinetochores in budding yeast is driven by Mps1 kinase-mediated phosphorylation of the MELT repeats of Spc105 and of Bub1. This phosphorylation drives the sequential recruitment of the Bub3–Bub1 and Mad1–Mad2 complexes to generate a cell-division arrest signal. This study quantitatively analyzed the number of Bub1 and Mad1 molecules recruited by a single unattached sister kinetochore pair. The authors revealed that two Mad1–Mad2 heterotetramers are recruited per kinetochore-bound Bub1 molecule, and demonstrated that two mechanisms—low intracellular Bub1 abundance and steric hindrance—define the amount of SAC proteins bound to Spc105.

### `SCE-CC-N31`: `MPS1` → `BUB1` (`phosphorylation/activation`)
- **TSV Description**: Kinetochore-bound Bub1 is also phosphorylated by Mps1, which lets Mad1-Mad2 bind.
- **Location**: Abstract / Introduction
- **Extracted Phrases/Sentences**:
  > "Recruitment of spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) proteins by an unattached kinetochore leads to SAC activation. This recruitment is licensed by the Mps1 kinase, which phosphorylates the kinetochore protein Spc105 at one or more of its six MELT repeats. Spc105 then recruits the Bub3-Bub1 and Mad1-Mad2 complexes, which produce the inhibitory signal that arrests cell division."
- **Evidence Explanation**: Citing London & Biggins (2014) and Moyle et al. (2014), this paper (PMC5221577) quantitatively examines the mechanism by which Mps1 kinase phosphorylates Spc105 and kinetochore-bound Bub1 at unattached kinetochores to license the binding of the Bub3–Bub1 and Mad1–Mad2 complexes and generate the SAC signal.

### `SCE-CC-N32`: `BUB1` → `MAD1` (`binding (recruitment)`)
- **TSV Description**: Phosphorylated Bub1 recruits Mad1-Mad2.
- **Location**: Abstract / Results
- **Extracted Phrases/Sentences**:

- **Evidence Explanation**: This study quantitatively demonstrates that phosphorylated Bub1 (the Bub3–Bub1 complex) at the kinetochore recruits the Mad1–Mad2 complex to the kinetochore (recruiting two Mad1–Mad2 heterotetramers for every Bub3–Bub1 molecule), directly supporting `SCE-CC-N32`.

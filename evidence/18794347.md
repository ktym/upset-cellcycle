# PCNA-dependent regulation of p21 ubiquitylation and degradation via the CRL4Cdt2 ubiquitin ligase complex

- **PMID**: 18794347

- **Journal**: Genes & Development (2008) 22:2496-2506
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.1676108

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N14` (`DTL|DDB1|CUL4A|PCNA` → `CDT1|CDKN1A|KMT5A`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The CDK inhibitor p21 (CDKN1A) is a key regulator of cell-cycle progression and DNA replication that must be rapidly degraded during S phase and in response to DNA damage. This study demonstrates that p21 bound to the replication clamp PCNA is recognized by the CRL4-Cdt2 E3 ubiquitin ligase complex—composed of CUL4, DDB1, and Cdt2 (DTL)—and undergoes ubiquitylation on chromatin followed by proteasomal degradation. This degradation requires direct binding to PCNA via the PIP box on p21 as well as the substrate receptor Cdt2 (DTL); similar to Cdt1 degradation in S phase, CRL4-Cdt2 degrades PCNA-bound p21 to prevent DNA re-replication and control S-phase progression.

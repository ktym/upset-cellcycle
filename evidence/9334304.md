# CDC20 and CDH1: a family of substrate-specific activators of APC-dependent proteolysis

- **PMID**: 9334304
- **Authors**: Visintin R, Prinz S, Amon A
- **Journal**: Science (1997) 278:460-463
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.278.5337.460
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9334304/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-037` (`CDC20|CDC16|CDC23|CDC27` → `PDS1`), `SCE-CC-047` (`CDH1` → `CLB2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Proteolysis mediated by the anaphase-promoting complex (APC/C) triggers chromosome segregation and mitotic exit, but the mechanism of its substrate-specific regulation had remained unknown. In this study, the evolutionarily conserved proteins Cdc20 and Cdh1 (Hct1) were identified as rate-limiting, substrate-specific activators of APC-dependent proteolysis. Cdc20 was essential for degradation of the anaphase inhibitor Pds1, but not for degradation of Clb2 or Ase1. Conversely, cdh1 deletion mutants were impaired in the degradation of Clb2 and Ase1, whereas Pds1 degradation was normal. Furthermore, overexpression of either CDC20 or CDH1 was sufficient to induce APC-dependent degradation of their respective specific substrates (Pds1 or Clb2/Ase1) even during cell-cycle stages when those substrates are normally stable.

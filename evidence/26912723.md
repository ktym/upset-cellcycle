# Phosphopeptide binding by Sld3 links Dbf4-dependent kinase to MCM replicative helicase activation

- **PMID**: 26912723
- **Authors**: Tom D. Deegan, Joseph T. P. Yeeles, John F. X. Diffley
- **Journal**: The EMBO Journal (2016) 35:961-973
- **DOI**: 10.15252/embj.201593552
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26912723/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N15` (`SLD3|SLD7` → `MCM4|MCM6`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates how phosphorylation of the MCM complex by Dbf4-dependent kinase (DDK: Cdc7-Dbf4) is translated into CMG helicase activation during eukaryotic DNA replication initiation. The replication initiation factor Sld3 (in complex with Sld7) acts as a specific "reader" that recognizes and binds DDK-phosphorylated N-terminal peptides of Mcm4 and Mcm6, thereby recruiting Cdc45 onto the inactive MCM double hexamer. Phosphomimicking mutants of Mcm4 and Mcm6 bind Sld3 and initiate replication even in the absence of DDK, proving that direct recognition of phosphorylated Mcm4/Mcm6 by Sld3-Sld7 is the core mechanism of DDK-dependent replication initiation.

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Initiation and maintenance of mitosis require high cyclin B-Cdk1 activity alongside the suppression of counteracting protein phosphatase 2A (PP2A), a process dependent on Greatwall (MASTL in humans) kinase. Using biochemical purification and mass spectrometry, this study identified cAMP-regulated phosphoprotein 19 (Arpp19) and alpha-endosulfine (ENSA) as physiological substrates of Greatwall kinase. Greatwall directly phosphorylates a conserved serine residue (Ser62 in Arpp19 and the corresponding site in ENSA), and phosphorylated Arpp19 associates with and inhibits PP2A activity to control mitotic entry and maintenance.

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The transcription factor E2F is a key determinant of the G1/S transition in the mammalian cell cycle, activating the transcription of genes required for DNA replication. In this study, the accumulation of the G1 cyclin, cyclin E (CCNE1), was demonstrated to be directly regulated by E2F1. Functional E2F-binding sites are present in the cyclin E promoter, and expression of E2F1 activates transcription of the endogenous cyclin E gene in a cell-cycle-dependent manner, revealing that E2F1 and cyclin E form a positive autoregulatory feedback loop that drives G1 progression.

# Mammalian Cdc7-Dbf4 protein kinase complex is essential for initiation of DNA replication

- **PMID**: 10523313
- **Authors**: W. Jiang, D. McDonald, T. J. Hope, T. Hunter
- **Journal**: The EMBO Journal (1999) 18:5703-5713
- **DOI**: 10.1093/emboj/18.20.5703
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10523313/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-023` (`CDC7|DBF4` → `MCM2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study isolated the human homolog of budding yeast Dbf4 (HsDbf4 / DBF4) and revealed that it binds to human Cdc7 kinase (HsCdc7 / CDC7) to form an active kinase complex (DDK). HsDbf4 protein levels and HsCdc7–HsDbf4 kinase activity increase at the G1/S transition and directly phosphorylate MCM2, a component of the pre-replicative complex (pre-RC). Immunoneutralization by microinjection of anti-HsCdc7 antibodies blocked the initiation of DNA replication in mammalian cells, demonstrating that MCM phosphorylation by the Cdc7–Dbf4 complex is essential for replication origin firing (initiation of DNA replication) in S phase.

# A complex of Cdc4p, Skp1p, and Cdc53p/cullin catalyzes ubiquitination of the phosphorylated CDK inhibitor Sic1p

- **PMID**: 9346239
- **Authors**: R. M. Feldman, C. C. Correll, K. B. Kaplan, R. J. Deshaies
- **Journal**: Cell (1997) 91:221-230
- **DOI**: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80404-3
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9346239/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-013` (`CDC4|SKP1|CDC53|CDC34` → `SIC1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The G1/S transition in budding yeast requires Cdc4p, Cdc34p, Cdc53p, Skp1p, and Cln/Cdc28p kinase, which act together to promote degradation of the S-phase CDK inhibitor Sic1p. In this study, the F-box protein Cdc4p, the cullin homolog Cdc53p, and Skp1p were shown to assemble into a complex functioning as the ubiquitin ligase SCF(Cdc4p). In an in vitro reconstitution system using purified proteins, combining the SCF(Cdc4p) complex, E1 enzyme, the E2 enzyme Cdc34p, and ubiquitin completely reconstituted polyubiquitination of Sic1p phosphorylated by Cln/Cdc28p. Furthermore, the Cdc4p/Skp1p subcomplex was shown to bind specifically via its WD40 repeats only to phosphorylated Sic1p, and not to unphosphorylated Sic1p.

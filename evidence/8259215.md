# A new regulatory motif in cell-cycle control causing specific inhibition of cyclin D/CDK4

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The eukaryotic cell division cycle is regulated by cyclin-dependent kinases (CDKs), and complexes of CDK4 with D-type cyclins are involved in passage through G1 phase. This study isolated the human p16 (CDKN2A / p16INK4a) cDNA and revealed that the p16 protein binds directly to CDK4 to competitively prevent complex formation with cyclin D, thereby specifically inhibiting the catalytic activity of CDK4/cyclin D enzymes. p16 functions as an important component of a regulatory feedback circuit involving CDK4, D-type cyclins, and the retinoblastoma protein (pRb).

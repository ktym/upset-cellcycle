- **DOI**: 10.1038/emboj.2012.28
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22333915/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N19` (`RAD53` → `NRM1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates how the S-phase checkpoint activated in response to DNA replication stress (such as hydroxyurea or MMS treatment) regulates the G1/S transcriptional program. Normally, upon entry into S phase, SBF target genes are repressed by Clb-CDK and MBF target genes are repressed by binding of the corepressor Nrm1. Under replication stress, however, the checkpoint effector kinase Rad53 directly phosphorylates and inactivates Nrm1, preventing its binding to MBF target gene promoters. Consequently, transcriptional repression of MBF target genes essential for DNA replication and repair is relieved, sustaining G1/S transcription under the S-phase checkpoint.

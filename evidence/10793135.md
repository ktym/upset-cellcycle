# Mitotic regulation of the APC activator proteins CDC20 and CDH1

- **PMID**: 10793135
- **Authors**: Edgar R. Kramer, Nadja Scheuringer, Alexandre V. Podtelejnikov, Matthias Mann, Jan-Michael Peters
- **Journal**: Mol Biol Cell (2000) 11:1555-1569
- **DOI**: 10.1091/mbc.11.5.1555
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10793135/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-026` (`CCNA2|CDK2` → `FZR1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Sequential activation of the anaphase-promoting complex (APC/C) by CDC20 and CDH1 (FZR1) is essential for the onset of anaphase and exit from mitosis, respectively. This study showed that whereas CDC20 binds and activates only mitotically phosphorylated APC, CDH1 is phosphorylated in vivo throughout S, G2, and M phases. In vitro and in vivo analyses demonstrated that phosphorylated CDH1 exhibits markedly reduced ability to bind to and activate the APC compared with non-phosphorylated CDH1, and that non-phosphorylatable CDH1 mutants constitutively activate the APC whereas phosphomimetic mutants are constitutively inactive. Thus, phosphorylation of CDH1 by cyclin-dependent kinases blocks CDH1 binding to the APC, preventing premature activation of APC–CDH1 from S to M phase.

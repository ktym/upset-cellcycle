- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23579499/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N40` (`CDC15` → `NUD1`), `SCE-CC-N41` (`NUD1` → `DBF2|MOB1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The budding yeast mitotic exit network (MEN; the ortholog of the metazoan Hippo tumor-suppressor pathway) is a scaffold-dependent kinase cascade that governs the transition from mitosis to G1. This study elucidates that signal transduction in the MEN kinase cascade proceeds via two-step phosphorylation-dependent complex assembly. First, the MEN kinase Cdc15 phosphorylates the SPB scaffold protein Nud1 to create a phospho-docking site; second, the effector kinase complex Dbf2-Mob1 binds phosphorylated Nud1 via the phosphoserine/threonine-binding domain of Mob1 and is recruited to the SPB, enabling Cdc15 to activate Dbf2-Mob1.

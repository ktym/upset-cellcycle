## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Combining mathematical modeling and in vivo experiments, this study analyzes how the stress-activated protein kinase Hog1 exercises time-dependent, quantitative control over multiple components of the G1/S transition network to induce G1 arrest upon hyperosmotic stress. Hog1 represses the transcription of CLN1/2 and CLB5 while directly phosphorylating and stabilizing the CDK inhibitor Sic1 at Thr173. Particularly in cells subjected to osmostress near Start, Hog1-mediated phosphorylation and stabilization of Sic1 are quantitatively demonstrated to be essential for preventing premature initiation of DNA replication driven by already accumulating Clb5-Cdc28 activity.

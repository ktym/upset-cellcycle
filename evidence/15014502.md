# Control of the SCF(Skp2-Cks1) ubiquitin ligase by the APC/C(Cdh1) ubiquitin ligase

- **PMID**: 15014502
- **Authors**: Tarig Bashir, N. Valerio Dorrello, Virginia Amador, Daniele Guardavaccaro, Michele Pagano
- **Journal**: Nature (2004) 428:190-193
- **DOI**: 10.1038/nature02330
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15014502/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-053` (`FZR1` → `SKP2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The F-box protein Skp2 (SKP2) and its cofactor Cks1 are the substrate-recognition subunits of the SCF(Skp2-Cks1) ubiquitin ligase complex that ubiquitinates and degrades the CDK inhibitors p27 (CDKN1B) and p21 to promote S-phase entry. This study revealed that both Skp2 and Cks1 proteins are unstable in G1 phase and that their degradation is directly mediated by the ubiquitin ligase APC/C-Cdh1, composed of the anaphase-promoting complex APC/C and its activator Cdh1 (FZR1). Knockdown of Cdh1 by RNAi in G1 cells stabilized Skp2 and Cks1, resulting in enhanced proteolysis of p27 and p21 and premature entry into S phase. Thus, degradation of Skp2 by APC/C-Cdh1 is a principal mechanism that keeps p27 stable in G1 phase and maintains the G1 state.

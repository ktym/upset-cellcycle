## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Wild-type p53 functions as a transcription factor that suppresses cell cycle progression in response to cues such as DNA damage. Using subtractive hybridization, this study isolated and identified WAF1 (CDKN1A / p21) as a gene directly induced at the transcriptional level by wild-type p53. The WAF1 promoter region contains a p53-binding site and is strongly transcriptionally activated by wild-type p53 (but not mutant p53), and introduction of WAF1 suppresses tumor cell growth, demonstrating that it is a direct mediator of p53-induced cell cycle arrest.

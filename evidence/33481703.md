## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The Mitotic Exit Network (MEN) in budding yeast must transmit a signal generated at the cytoplasmic face of spindle pole bodies (SPBs) across the nuclear envelope to the effector phosphatase Cdc14 sequestered in the nucleolus. This study (PMC7822594) revealed that the terminal kinase complex Dbf2–Mob1, once activated by Cdc15 at the SPB, translocates into the nucleus and is recruited to the nucleolus guided by Polo-like kinase Cdc5-mediated priming phosphorylation of Cfi1/Net1. Once in the nucleolus, Dbf2–Mob1 directly phosphorylates the Cdc14 inhibitor Cfi1/Net1 (as well as Cdc14), triggering the dissociation and release of Cdc14 from the nucleolus.

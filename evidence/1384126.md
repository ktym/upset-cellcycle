# Inactivation of the p34cdc2-cyclin B complex by the human WEE1 tyrosine kinase

- **PMID**: 1384126
- **Authors**: Laura L. Parker, Helen Piwnica-Worms
- **Journal**: Science (1992) 257:1955-1957
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1384126

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In eukaryotic cells, entry into mitosis (M phase) is governed by the kinase activity of the M-phase promoting factor (MPF), the p34cdc2 (CDK1)-cyclin B complex. This study purified and characterized the human WEE1 protein, demonstrating that it functions as a tyrosine-specific protein kinase that directly phosphorylates and inactivates the p34cdc2-cyclin B complex. Specifically, human WEE1 specifically phosphorylates tyrosine residue 15 (Tyr15 / Y15) of p34cdc2, thereby inhibiting the histone H1 kinase activity of the p34cdc2-cyclin B complex and preventing premature transition from G2 phase into M phase.

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Human RIF1 (Rap1-interacting factor 1) interacts with protein phosphatase 1 (PP1) and plays two opposing yet critical roles in controlling DNA replication. In S phase, the RIF1–PP1 complex counteracts DDK-dependent phosphorylation of the replicative MCM helicase complex (particularly the regulatory N-terminal domain of MCM4) through dephosphorylation, thereby suppressing excessive or premature origin activation (firing). Conversely, in G1 phase, RIF1–PP1 protects the origin-binding protein ORC1 from untimely phosphorylation and subsequent proteasomal degradation, stabilizing ORC1 to stimulate DNA replication origin licensing (MCM complex loading).

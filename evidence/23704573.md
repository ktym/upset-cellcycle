# Identification of a heteromeric complex that promotes DNA replication origin firing in human cells

- **PMID**: 23704573
- **Authors**: Dominik Boos, Mona Yekezare, John F. X. Diffley
- **Journal**: Science (2013) 340:981-984
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1237448
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23704573/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N12` (`TICRR|MTBP` → `TOPBP1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Treslin/TICRR, the human ortholog of yeast Sld3, is an essential DNA replication initiation factor regulated by cyclin-dependent kinases (CDKs) and the DNA damage checkpoint. This study discovered that MTBP, previously known as an MDM2-binding protein, interacts with Treslin/TICRR throughout the cell cycle to form a heteromeric complex. Depletion of MTBP by siRNA blocks DNA replication by preventing the assembly of the CMG (Cdc45-MCM-GINS) holohelicase during origin firing. Independent of p53 status, MTBP collaborates with Treslin/TICRR and TopBP1 to integrate cell-cycle and DNA damage response signals and promote DNA replication origin firing in human cells.

# CDK activity antagonizes Whi5, an inhibitor of G1/S transcription in yeast

- **PMID**: 15210111
- **Authors**: Michael Costanzo, Joy L. Nishikawa, Xiaojing Tang, Jonathan S. Millman, Oliver Schub, Kevin Breitkreuz, Danielle Dewar, Ivan Rupes, Brenda Andrews, Mike Tyers
- **Journal**: Cell (2004) 117:899-913
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cell.2004.05.024
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15210111/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-002` (`CLN3|CDC28` → `WHI5`), `SCE-CC-003` (`WHI5` → `SWI4|SWI6`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Whi5, identified as a cell-size regulator in budding yeast, is a G1/S transcriptional repressor functionally analogous to the mammalian tumor suppressor protein Rb. During early G1 phase, Whi5 physically interacts with the transcription factors SBF (Swi4-Swi6) and MBF (Mbp1-Swi6) to be recruited to G1/S-specific promoters and repress transcription. In late G1 phase, CDK activity centered on Cln3-Cdc28 phosphorylates Whi5 at multiple sites, inducing its dissociation from SBF and nuclear export to relieve transcriptional repression and drive passage through Start. When CDK activity declines at the end of M phase, Whi5 re-enters the nucleus and returns G1/S transcription to a repressed state.

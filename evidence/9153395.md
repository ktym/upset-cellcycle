# Mdm2 promotes the rapid degradation of p53

- **PMID**: 9153395
- **Authors**: Y. Haupt, R. Maya, A. Kazaz, M. Oren
- **Journal**: Nature (1997) 387:296-299
- **DOI**: 10.1038/387296a0

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-013` (`MDM2` → `TP53`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The tumor suppressor protein p53 induces cell-cycle arrest or apoptosis in response to various stresses, and its activity is negatively regulated by the oncoprotein Mdm2. In this study, Mdm2 was shown not only to inhibit the transcriptional transactivation ability of p53, but also to bind directly to p53 and promote its rapid proteasome-mediated degradation. During recovery from DNA damage, induction of Mdm2 coincides with the rapid disappearance of p53, functioning as a regulatory mechanism by which Mdm2-promoted p53 degradation ensures termination of the p53 signal.

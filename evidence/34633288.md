## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The spindle position checkpoint (SPOC) in budding yeast is a surveillance mechanism that prevents mitotic exit when the spindle is misoriented, thereby preserving genome integrity. This study identified the type 1 protein phosphatase Glc7 (PP1) in complex with its regulatory subunit Bud14 (Glc7–Bud14) as a novel SPOC component that promotes dephosphorylation of the SPOC effector Bfa1. A robust checkpoint response requires two mechanisms acting in parallel: first, the SPOC kinase Kin4 phosphorylates Bfa1 to isolate it away from the inhibitory kinase Cdc5; second, Glc7–Bud14 dephosphorylates Cdc5-phosphorylated Bfa1 to fully activate the Bfa1–Bub2 complex.

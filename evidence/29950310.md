# Cdc48/p97 segregase is modulated by cyclin-dependent kinase to determine cyclin fate during G1 progression

- **PMID**: 29950310
- **Authors**: Eva Parisi, Galal Yahya, Alba Flores, Martí Aldea
- **Journal**: The EMBO Journal (2018) 37:e98724
- **DOI**: 10.15252/embj.201798724
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29950310/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N05` (`CDC48` → `CLN3`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidated the mechanism by which the AAA+ ATPase segregase Cdc48 (homolog of mammalian p97/VCP) determines the fate of the G1 cyclin Cln3 (degradation versus release and nuclear import) during G1 progression. Cdc48 prevents the immediate degradation of ubiquitinated Cln3 while stimulating its release from the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) and its nuclear accumulation to drive cell-cycle entry (Start). Furthermore, phosphorylation of Cdc48 itself by CDK (Cdc28) at conserved sites modulates cofactor recruitment, functioning as a positive feedback mechanism that rapidly switches G1 cyclin abundance and activity.

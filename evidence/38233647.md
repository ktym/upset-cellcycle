# The CDK Pho85 inhibits Whi7 Start repressor to promote cell cycle entry in budding yeast

- **PMID**: 38233647

- **Journal**: EMBO Reports (2024) 25:745-769
- **DOI**: 10.1038/s44319-023-00049-7
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38233647/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N03` (`PHO85` → `WHI7`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study revealed that the environmentally responsive cyclin-dependent kinase Pho85 phosphorylates and inhibits the Start transcriptional repressor Whi7 to promote cell-cycle entry (G1/S transition) in budding yeast. Pho85 negatively regulates Whi7 through a multilayered mechanism: it directly phosphorylates Whi7 to induce Whi7 protein instability (promoting degradation), represses `WHI7` gene transcription, and reduces the intrinsic ability of Whi7 to bind promoters. Furthermore, Whi7 acts as the primary repressor driving G1 arrest in Pho85-deficient (`pho85` mutant) cells, demonstrating that Pho85 controls Start progression in response to nutrient and stress environments via phosphorylation and inhibition of Whi7.

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

A 40 kDa protein (p40) coprecipitating with immunoprecipitates of the budding yeast cyclin-dependent kinase p34CDC28 (Cdc28) was biochemically purified, and its encoding gene was cloned and identified as SIC1 (Substrate/Subunit Inhibitor of Cdc28). Purified p40 (Sic1) functions as an endogenous inhibitor (CKI) that stoichiometrically and potently inhibits Cdc28 protein kinase activity in vitro. Furthermore, although SIC1-deleted strains are viable, they exhibit a high frequency of chromosome loss and mitotic abnormalities, demonstrating for the first time that negative regulation of Cdc28 kinase activity by Sic1 is important for genome stability and normal cell cycle progression.

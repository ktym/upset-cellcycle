## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The AAA+ ATPase TRIP13 acts together with the adaptor protein p31comet (MAD2L1BP) to remodel the HORMA-domain protein MAD2 from its closed conformation (C-MAD2) to its open conformation (O-MAD2), thereby promoting disassembly of the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC) and regulating the spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC). This study discovered that biallelic loss-of-function mutations in TRIP13 predispose individuals to Wilms tumor and chromosome segregation defects resembling mosaic variegated aneuploidy (MVA) syndrome. In patient-derived cells, loss of TRIP13 function impairs the conformational conversion of C-MAD2 to O-MAD2 and MCC turnover, resulting in severe SAC dysfunction and a high frequency of chromosome missegregation.

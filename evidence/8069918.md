# A novel cyclin associates with MO15/CDK7 to form the CDK-activating kinase

- **PMID**: 8069918

- **Journal**: Cell (1994) 78:713-724

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Full activation of cyclin-dependent kinases (CDKs) requires phosphorylation of a conserved threonine residue within the T-loop by the CDK-activating kinase (CAK). In this study, CAK was purified from mammalian cells and shown to consist of a 42 kDa catalytic subunit, MO15/CDK7, and a novel 37 kDa regulatory subunit, cyclin H (CCNH). In vitro reconstituted CDK7-cyclin H complexes phosphorylate and activate CDK2 and CDC2 (CDK1) bound to various cyclins.

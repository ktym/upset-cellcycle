## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Extracellular antimitogenic signals such as cell contact inhibition, TGF-β, and cAMP arrest the cell cycle by preventing the activation of G1 cyclin-dependent kinases (CDKs). In this study, the cDNA for p27Kip1 (CDKN1B), a heat-stable protein that binds to and inactivates cyclin E-Cdk2 complexes, was cloned. p27Kip1 inhibits the activity of cyclin E-Cdk2, cyclin A-Cdk2, and cyclin D-Cdk4 complexes as well as the activating phosphorylation of Cdk2, and its overexpression arrests cells in G1 phase.

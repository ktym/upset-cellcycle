# Mechanisms that help the yeast cell cycle clock tick: G2 cyclins transcriptionally activate G2 cyclins and repress G1 cyclins

- **PMID**: 8402888
- **Authors**: A. Amon, M. Tyers, B. Futcher, K. Nasmyth
- **Journal**: Cell (1993) 74:993-1007
- **DOI**: 10.1016/0092-8674(93)90722-3
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8402888/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-029` (`CLB2|CDC28` → `SWI4`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

During cell cycle progression in budding yeast, G1 cyclins (CLN1 and CLN2) are expressed in G1 to S phase, whereas mitotic cyclins (CLB1 and CLB2) are expressed in G2 to M phase. This study revealed that the G2/M cyclins Clb1/Clb2 activate their own transcription via positive feedback while potently repressing the transcription of the G1 cyclins CLN1 and CLN2 in G2 phase, thereby driving irreversible cell cycle transitions. This negative regulation by Clb cyclins does not act on MCB-regulated genes (such as CLB5) but is specific to SCB-regulated genes (CLN1 and CLN2) and is mediated through the transcription factor Swi4. Indeed, it was demonstrated that the Swi4 protein forms a complex with Clb2 in vivo and serves as an efficient substrate for the Clb2-Cdc28 kinase in vitro.

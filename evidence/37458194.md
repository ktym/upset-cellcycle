- **Journal**: The EMBO Journal (2023) 42:e114131

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

During the initiation of chromosomal DNA replication in eukaryotes, Cdc45 and the GINS complex bind to MCM2–7 hexamers loaded at replication origins to form the active CMG (Cdc45–MCM2–7–GINS) helicase, but the molecular mechanism bridging GINS and TopBP1 to assemble CMG in vertebrates had remained unknown. Using *Xenopus* egg extracts, this study demonstrated that DONSON—the gene product mutated in microcephalic primordial dwarfism (such as Meier–Gorlin syndrome)—is an essential factor for CMG helicase assembly during vertebrate DNA replication initiation. DONSON interacts with GINS and TopBP1 to promote their recruitment onto chromatin-bound MCM2–7, driving CDK- and DDK-dependent assembly of the CMG helicase.

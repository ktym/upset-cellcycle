# Downregulation of the Tem1 GTPase by Amn1 after cytokinesis involves both nuclear import and SCF-mediated degradation

- **PMID**: 34518877
- **Authors**: Alain Devault, Simonetta Piatti
- **Journal**: Journal of Cell Science (2021) 134:jcs258972
- **DOI**: 10.1242/jcs.258972
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34518877/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N44` (`AMN1` → `TEM1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The Mitotic Exit Network (MEN) in budding yeast is activated by the Tem1 GTPase at spindle pole bodies (SPBs), but MEN activity must be rapidly turned off once mitotic exit and cytokinesis are completed. This study revealed that the Amn1 protein, which is asymmetrically expressed in daughter cells, negatively regulates Tem1 after cytokinesis through a dual mode of action. First, Amn1 evicts Tem1 from SPBs and escorts it into the nucleus; second, Amn1 acts as an F-box subunit of the SCF ubiquitin ligase complex to promote the ubiquitination and degradation of Tem1.

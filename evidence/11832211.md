# Separase, polo kinase, the kinetochore protein Slk19, and Spo12 function in a network that controls Cdc14 localization during early anaphase

- **PMID**: 11832211

- **Journal**: Cell (2002) 108:207-220
- **DOI**: 10.1016/s0092-8674(01)00634-1

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The budding yeast protein phosphatase Cdc14 is essential for mitotic exit and is sequestered in the nucleolus from G1 through metaphase by binding to its inhibitor Net1 (Cfi1). Previously, the release of Cdc14 from the nucleolus was thought to be controlled solely by the Mitotic Exit Network (MEN) acting in late anaphase. This study discovered a novel regulatory pathway that promotes transient release of Cdc14 from the nucleolus during early anaphase, named the FEAR (Cdc Fourteen Early Anaphase Release) network. The FEAR network consists of separase (Esp1), Polo-like kinase (Cdc5), the kinetochore protein Slk19, and Spo12, and it was shown that Cdc14 released in early anaphase activates the MEN to ensure efficient mitotic exit.

# The SBF- and MBF-associated protein Msa1 is required for proper timing of G1-specific transcription in Saccharomyces cerevisiae

- **PMID**: 18160399

- **Journal**: Journal of Biological Chemistry (2008) 283:6040-6049
- **DOI**: 10.1074/jbc.M708248200

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study analyzes the regulation of G1-specific transcription by Msa1, one of a pair of homologous proteins, Msa1 (Yor066w) and Msa2 (Ykr077w), identified by proteomic screening as novel interactors of SBF (Swi4-Swi6) and MBF (Mbp1-Swi6). MSA1 is expressed from late M phase to early G1 phase and binds to target gene promoters during G1 in a manner dependent on its cognate transcription factor (SBF or MBF). Because overexpression of Msa1 advances the timing of SBF/MBF-dependent transcription and budding (Start entry) whereas deletion of Msa1 delays them, Msa1 is shown to bind SBF and MBF to positively regulate the timing of G1/S transcriptional initiation.

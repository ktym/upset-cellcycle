# Mad1 kinetochore recruitment by Mps1-mediated phosphorylation of Bub1 signals the spindle checkpoint

- **PMID**: 24402315
- **Authors**: Nitobe London, Sue Biggins
- **Journal**: Genes & Development (2014) 28:140-152
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.233700.113
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24402315/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N31` (`MPS1` → `BUB1`), `SCE-CC-N32` (`BUB1` → `MAD1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Activation of the spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) requires hierarchical recruitment of checkpoint proteins to unattached kinetochores followed by downstream kinetochore association of the Mad1-Mad2 complex. This study demonstrates that in budding yeast, Mps1 kinase directly phosphorylates the middle region of Bub1 in addition to Spc105, and phosphorylated Bub1 directly binds the Mad1-Mad2 complex to recruit it to kinetochores. Artificial tethering of this Bub1 region to kinetochores bypasses the requirement for upstream Mps1-dependent Spc105 phosphorylation, and binding among Mps1, Bub1, Mad1, and Mad2 can be reconstituted, demonstrating that Mps1-mediated Bub1 phosphorylation and subsequent Mad1 recruitment constitute a critical step in SAC signaling.

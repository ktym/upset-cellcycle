- **Journal**: Genes & Development (2011) 25:801-813
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.2034211

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In the absence of proliferative signals, cells exit the cell cycle and enter G0 (quiescence), a state in which the DREAM (DP, RB-like, E2F, and MuvB) complex transcriptionally represses cell-cycle-dependent genes. The DREAM complex consists of p130 (RBL2), E2F4, DP1, and five MuvB core proteins (LIN9, LIN37, LIN52, LIN54, and RBBP4), whereas upon cell-cycle entry the MuvB core dissociates from p130 and associates with B-MYB. Using mass spectrometry to analyze the regulatory mechanism of DREAM complex assembly by the MuvB core, this study discovered that the dual-specificity protein kinase DYRK1A specifically phosphorylates LIN52 on serine residue 28 (Ser28). This LIN52-S28 phosphorylation is essential for binding between the MuvB core and p130/p107 and for DREAM complex assembly; inhibiting DYRK1A or mutating LIN52-S28 impairs entry into quiescence and Ras-induced senescence.

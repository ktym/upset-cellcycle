# CLB5 and CLB6, a new pair of B cyclins involved in DNA replication in Saccharomyces cerevisiae

- **PMID**: 8319908
- **Authors**: E. Schwob, K. Nasmyth
- **Journal**: Genes & Development (1993) 7:1160-1175
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.7.7a.1160

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study identified CLB5 and CLB6 as a novel pair of B-type cyclins responsible for the initiation and progression of DNA replication (S phase) in budding yeast. CLB5 and CLB6 mRNAs possess MCB (MluI cell cycle box) elements in their promoter regions and are periodically transcriptionally induced at the G1/S boundary in an MBF-dependent and Cln activity-dependent manner. In clb5 clb6 double-mutant strains, budding occurs normally, but a marked delay in S-phase initiation and reduced progression of DNA replication are observed. Furthermore, ectopic expression of CLB5 bypasses the lethality of G1 cyclin (Cln1/2/3)-deficient strains and allows cell proliferation, demonstrating that Clb5/Clb6-Cdc28 kinases induced in a Cln-dependent manner are direct executors of S-phase initiation.

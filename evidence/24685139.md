# Rif1 controls DNA replication timing in yeast through the PP1 phosphatase Glc7

- **PMID**: 24685139
- **Authors**: Stefano Mattarocci, Maksym Shyian, Laure Lemmens, Pascal Damay, Dogus Murat Altintas, Tianlai Shi, Clinton R. Bartholomew, Nicolas H. Thomä, Christopher F. J. Hardy, David Shore
- **Journal**: Cell Reports (2014) 7:62-69
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.celrep.2014.03.010
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24685139/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N13` (`RIF1|GLC7` → `MCM4`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study demonstrates that budding yeast Rif1 inhibits activation of pre-replication complexes (pre-RCs) and controls DNA replication timing by recruiting the PP1 phosphatase Glc7. The N-terminal RVxF and SILK motifs of Rif1 are essential for its physical interaction with Glc7, and DDK-dependent phosphorylation of Mcm4 (and Sld3) in G1 phase is elevated in rif1 mutants. Furthermore, Rif1 recruits Glc7 to telomeric regions and other loci to restrain premature replication initiation, establishing that Rif1-Glc7-mediated Mcm4 dephosphorylation functions as a negative regulatory mechanism for replication origin firing.

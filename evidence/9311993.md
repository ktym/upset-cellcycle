# Phosphorylation-dependent degradation of the cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor p27Kip1

- **PMID**: 9311993
- **Authors**: J. Vlach, S. Hennecke, B. Amati
- **Journal**: The EMBO Journal (1997) 16:5334-5344
- **DOI**: 10.1093/emboj/16.17.5334

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-020` (`CCNE1|CDK2` → `CDKN1B`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

p27Kip1 (CDKN1B) binds to G1-specific cyclin-CDK complexes to inhibit their catalytic activity, and is also regulated by degradation via the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway. In this study, active cyclin E-CDK2 was shown to efficiently phosphorylate a conserved C-terminal CDK target motif (TPKK, containing Thr-187) on p27 and induce its rapid proteasome-mediated degradation. Mutation of this Thr-187 site to valine (VPKK) prevents proteasomal degradation and stabilizes p27, establishing that T187 phosphorylation by cyclin E-CDK2 triggers p27 degradation.

# Regulation of retinoblastoma protein functions by ectopic expression of human cyclins

- **PMID**: 1388095

- **Journal**: Cell (1992) 70:993-1006
- **DOI**: 10.1016/0092-8674(92)90249-C

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-018` (`CCNE1|CDK2` → `RB1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The retinoblastoma protein (pRb) negatively regulates cell proliferation, and introducing the RB gene into SAOS-2 osteosarcoma cells lacking functional pRb arrests cell cycle progression. This study demonstrated that constitutive expression of human cyclin E and cyclin A induces hyperphosphorylation of pRb and overcomes pRb-mediated suppression of proliferation. This phosphorylation is essential for the rescue of pRb-induced cell cycle block by cyclin E, showing that the cyclin E-CDK complex directly phosphorylates and inactivates pRb.

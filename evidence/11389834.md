- **PMID**: 11389834

- **Journal**: Cell (2001) 105:645-655

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study newly identified Emi1 (FBXO5), an inhibitor of the anaphase-promoting complex (APC/C), and elucidated its role in cell-cycle regulation. Emi1 is a conserved F-box protein containing a zinc-binding region (ZBR) essential for APC/C inhibition; it accumulates prior to mitosis to prevent premature APC/C activation, allowing the accumulation of APC/C substrates such as cyclin B. Non-degradable Emi1 stabilizes APC/C substrates and potently inhibits APC/C activity, demonstrating that Emi1 functions as a central inhibitor suppressing the APC/C from G1/S phase through early mitosis.

# A cDNA encoding a pRB-binding protein with properties of the transcription factor E2F

- **Journal**: Cell (1992) 70:337-350

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The retinoblastoma protein (pRB) controls cell proliferation by binding to and regulating the transcription factor E2F. Based on its ability to interact with pRB, this study isolated and identified a cDNA encoding a protein with the properties of E2F (RBP3, later designated E2F1). This protein binds pRB both in vitro and in vivo, possesses the ability to bind E2F recognition sites in a sequence-specific manner to activate transcription, and has its transcriptional activation activity regulated through binding to pRB.

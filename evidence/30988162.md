- **PMID**: 30988162

- **Journal**: Life Science Alliance (2019) 2:e201800277
- **DOI**: 10.26508/lsa.201800277

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N04` (`YDJ1|SSA1` → `CLN3`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidated how a competitive network between molecular chaperones (Hsp70/Ssa1 and Hsp40/Ydj1) and client substrates regulates cell-cycle entry (Start) in response to cell growth rate and stress conditions. Release of the G1 cyclin Cln3 from the ER and its nuclear accumulation require available free chaperones (Ydj1/Ssa1), but unfolded proteins generated during protein synthesis or under stress competitively occupy these chaperones, restricting the nuclear import of Cln3. Mathematical modeling and quantitative analysis demonstrated that the availability of Ydj1/Ssa1 chaperones serves as a molecular mechanism directly linking cell growth and proteostasis/stress status to Cln3 activation and Start entry.

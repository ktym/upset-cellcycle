# p27, a novel inhibitor of G1 cyclin-Cdk protein kinase activity, is related to p21

- **PMID**: 8033213

- **Journal**: Cell (1994) 78:67-74
- **DOI**: 10.1016/0092-8674(94)90573-8

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Using a yeast interaction screen to search for proteins that interact with cyclin D1-Cdk4, a novel 27 kDa mouse CDK inhibitor protein, p27 (CDKN1B / p27Kip1), was identified. p27 shares high sequence homology in its N-terminal region with the Cdk inhibitor p21 (Cip1/Waf1), and binds to cyclin E-Cdk2, cyclin A-Cdk2, and cyclin D1-Cdk4 complexes to potently inhibit their histone H1 and Rb kinase activities. Overexpression of p27 blocks cell cycle entry into S phase and causes G1 arrest.

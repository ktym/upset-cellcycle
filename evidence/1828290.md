# Dephosphorylation and activation of a p34cdc2/cyclin B complex in vitro by human CDC25 protein

- **PMID**: 1828290
- **Authors**: Ulrich Strausfeld, Jean-Claude Labbé, Didier Fesquet, Jean-Claude Cavadore, André Picard, Kumar Sadhu, Paul Russell, Marcel Dorée
- **Journal**: Nature (1991) 351:242-245
- **DOI**: 10.1038/351242a0
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1828290/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-035` (`CDC25C|CDC25B` → `CDK1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Cells and oocytes arrested in the G2 phase of the cell cycle contain a p34cdc2/cyclin B complex that is maintained in an inactive form through phosphorylation of tyrosine and threonine residues on the p34cdc2 (CDK1) subunit. In this study, inactive p34cdc2/cyclin B complexes were purified from G2-arrested cells and subjected to in vitro experiments by adding purified recombinant bacterially expressed human homolog of the fission yeast cdc25+ gene product (p54CDC25H / CDC25C). Addition of human CDC25 protein directly triggered dephosphorylation of p34cdc2 and dramatically activated its histone H1 kinase activity. These findings support that human CDC25 removes inhibitory phosphorylation from the p34cdc2-cyclin B complex to directly activate it and drive the G2/M transition.

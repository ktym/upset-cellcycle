## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study revealed that the budding yeast G2 cyclin Clb3 specifically recognizes a proline-rich `PxxPxF` docking motif on substrates via its hydrophobic patch to drive G2-specific CDK substrate phosphorylation. Identified as a representative Clb3–Cdk1-specific substrate carrying this `PxxPxF` motif, Ypr174c (named Csa1: Cdc5 SPB anchor 1) is phosphorylated by Clb3–Cdc28 in a docking-dependent manner to create a binding (priming) site for the Polo-like kinase Cdc5, thereby promoting Cdc5 localization to the spindle pole body (SPB). Together, this work established a systematic framework for the docking specificities of the four major cell-cycle cyclin classes: G1 cyclins (`LP` motif), S cyclins (`RxL` motif), G2 cyclins (`PxxPxF` motif), and M cyclins (`LxF` motif).

# SKP2 is required for ubiquitin-mediated degradation of the CDK inhibitor p27

- **PMID**: 10559916
- **Authors**: A. C. Carrano, E. Eytan, A. Hershko, M. Pagano
- **Journal**: Nature Cell Biology (1999) 1:193-199
- **DOI**: 10.1038/12013

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Degradation of the mammalian CDK inhibitor p27 (CDKN1B) is essential for the cell cycle transition from quiescence to proliferation and for the G1/S transition. This study demonstrated that the F-box protein SKP2 (S-phase kinase-associated protein 2) specifically recognizes p27 in a Thr-187 phosphorylation-dependent manner and serves as the substrate-recognition subunit of the SCF (Skp1–Cullin 1–F-box) ubiquitin ligase complex to target p27 for ubiquitination and degradation. Inhibition or depletion of SKP2 suppresses p27 ubiquitination and degradation, causing G1 arrest.

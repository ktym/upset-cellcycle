- **Journal**: Cell (1991) 65:1053-1061

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The retinoblastoma susceptibility gene product (RB protein) is a key negative regulator of cell proliferation, and complex formation with the transcription factor E2F was demonstrated as its mechanism of action. Only the underphosphorylated (active) form of the RB protein is found within E2F complexes, and the adenovirus E1A protein inactivates the growth-suppressive function of RB by dissociating this E2F-RB complex. These results established that direct binding between RB and E2F is a central event in the control of the cell cycle and cell proliferation.

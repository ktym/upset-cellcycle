# In vitro regulation of budding yeast Bfa1/Bub2 GAP activity by Cdc5

- **PMID**: 12637549
- **Authors**: Geymonat M, Spanos A, Walker PA, Johnston LH, Sedgwick SG
- **Journal**: J Biol Chem (2003) 278:14591-14594
- **DOI**: 10.1074/jbc.C300059200
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12637549/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-056` (`CDC5` → `BFA1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The small GTPase Tem1 that drives the mitotic exit network (MEN) in budding yeast is negatively regulated by the Bfa1/Bub2 two-component GAP (GTPase-activating protein) complex. Although in vivo analyses had shown that Bfa1 is a phosphorylation target of the Polo-like kinase Cdc5, direct proof of its biochemical effect was needed. Using in vitro reconstitution assays with purified proteins, this study proved that Cdc5 directly phosphorylates Bfa1 and directly inhibits the GAP activity of the Bfa1/Bub2 complex toward Tem1 without impairing its physical binding to Tem1. This established that inhibition of Bfa1/Bub2 GAP activity by Cdc5 increases active Tem1-GTP to positively regulate mitotic exit.

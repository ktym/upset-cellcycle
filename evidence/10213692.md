# Reconstitution of G1 cyclin ubiquitination with complexes containing SCFGrr1 and Rbx1

- **PMID**: 10213692
- **Authors**: Dorota Skowyra, Deanna M. Koepp, Takumi Kamura, Melissa N. Conrad, Ronald C. Conaway, Joan W. Conaway, Stephen J. Elledge, J. Wade Harper
- **Journal**: Science (1999) 284:662-665
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.284.5414.662
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10213692/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-014` (`GRR1|SKP1|CDC53|HRT1|CDC34` → `CLN1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Budding yeast G1 cyclins (Cln1 and Cln2) are highly unstable proteins that are rapidly degraded via the SCF ubiquitin ligase pathway in a manner dependent on autophosphorylation by Cdc28. This study revealed that the RING finger protein Rbx1 (budding yeast gene name HRT1) is an essential catalytic core component of the SCF complex (Skp1–Cdc53–F-box protein), binding to the cullin subunit Cdc53 and the ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme (E2) Cdc34 to dramatically stimulate ubiquitin transfer. Furthermore, using purified SCF(Grr1)–Rbx1 complexes containing the F-box protein Grr1 together with Cdc34, the specific binding and polyubiquitination of Cln1 phosphorylated by Cln/Cdc28 were completely reconstituted in vitro.

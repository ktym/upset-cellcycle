# ORC and Cdc6p interact and determine the frequency of initiation of DNA replication in the genome

- **PMID**: 7774008
- **Authors**: Chun Liang, Michael Weinreich, Bruce Stillman
- **Journal**: Cell (1995) 81:667-676
- **DOI**: 10.1016/0092-8674(95)90528-6
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7774008/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-020` (`CDC6` → `ORC1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The origin recognition complex (ORC: a 6-subunit complex of Orc1 to Orc6) is an initiator factor that binds to replication origins (ARS) in budding yeast. In this study, the replication initiation factor CDC6 was isolated as a multicopy suppressor of the temperature-sensitive mutant orc5-1, and two-dimensional gel electrophoresis and biochemical analyses demonstrated that Cdc6p interacts both functionally and physically with ORC. Mutations in orc and cdc6 exhibit synthetic lethality, and recombinant Cdc6p binds directly to ORC (specifically complexes containing Orc1). These results indicate that ORC and Cdc6p form a prereplication complex (pre-RC) at individual replication origins during G1 phase to determine the frequency of DNA replication initiation across the genome.

# Far1 and Fus3 link the mating pheromone signal transduction pathway to three G1-phase Cdc28 kinase complexes

- **PMID**: 8395009
- **Authors**: M Tyers, B Futcher
- **Journal**: Molecular and Cellular Biology (1993) 13:5659-5669
- **DOI**: 10.1128/mcb.13.9.5659-5669.1993
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8395009/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-001` (`CLN3` → `CDC28`), `SCE-CC-009` (`FAR1` → `CDC28|CLN1|CLN2`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In budding yeast, the Cdc28 protein kinase controls commitment to cell division at Start in G1 phase and forms specific kinase complexes with each of the G1 cyclins Cln1, Cln2, and Cln3. Using epitope-tagged Cln1, Cln2, and Cln3, this study analyzed the activity and coprecipitated substrates of each Cdc28 complex, directly detecting and comparing all three G1 CDK complexes, including the Cln3-Cdc28 complex. Furthermore, it revealed that treatment with mating pheromone (α-factor) induces preferential association and phosphorylation of the CDK inhibitor Far1 with the Cln1-, Cln2-, and Cln3-Cdc28 kinase complexes in a manner dependent on the MAP kinase Fus3. These findings established the molecular basis by which the pheromone response pathway directly links to G1-phase Cdc28-Cln complexes to cause arrest at Start.

### `SCE-CC-001`: `CLN3` → `CDC28` (`binding/activation`)
- **TSV Description**: Cln3 forms a complex with Cdc28 and activates the kinase (the study also analysed Cln1/2/3 complexes and binding of Far1 and Fus3).
- **Location**: Title / Abstract
- **Extracted Phrases/Sentences**:
  > "We have studied the kinase complexes formed between Cdc28 and each of the G1 cyclins Cln1, Cln2, and Cln3. Each complex has a specific array of coprecipitated in vitro substrates."
- **Evidence Explanation**: Biochemically demonstrates that Cln3, like Cln1 and Cln2, physically forms a kinase complex with Cdc28 and functions as an active G1 CDK with distinct substrate phosphorylation activity.

- **Location**: Abstract
- **Extracted Phrases/Sentences**:

- **Evidence Explanation**: Shows that Far1, which is required for pheromone-induced arrest at Start, physically associates with Cdc28-Cln1/Cln2/Cln3 complexes upon α-factor treatment, supporting the physical basis for direct regulation of Cdc28-Cln complexes by Far1.

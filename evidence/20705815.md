# Survivin reads phosphorylated histone H3 threonine 3 to activate the mitotic kinase Aurora B

- **PMID**: 20705815
- **Authors**: Alexander E. Kelly, Cristina Ghenoiu, John Z. Xue, Christian Zierhut, Hiroshi Kimura, Hironori Funabiki
- **Journal**: Science (2010) 330:235-239
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1189505
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20705815/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N20` (`HASPIN` → `BIRC5|AURKB|INCENP`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Accurate chromosome segregation in mitosis requires that the chromosomal passenger complex (CPC), which contains the Aurora B kinase, be recruited to and activated on centromeric chromatin. This study reveals that phosphorylation of histone H3 at threonine 3 (H3T3ph), catalyzed by the kinase Haspin, is directly "read" (recognized) by the BIR domain of the CPC subunit Survivin (BIRC5). This specific recognition of H3T3ph by Survivin recruits the CPC to centromeric regions, driving activation of the mitotic kinase Aurora B as well as spindle assembly and chromosome alignment.

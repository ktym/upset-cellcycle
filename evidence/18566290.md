# Bora and the kinase Aurora A cooperatively activate the kinase Plk1 and control mitotic entry

- **PMID**: 18566290
- **Authors**: Akiko Seki, Judith A. Coppinger, Chang-Young Jang, John R. Yates III, Guowei Fang
- **Journal**: Science (2008) 320:1655-1658
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1157425

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N17` (`BORA|AURKA` → `PLK1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Polo-like kinase 1 (PLK1) is an essential kinase for mitotic entry and progression, yet the identity of the upstream kinase that directly phosphorylates and activates PLK1 during the G2/M transition remained unknown. This study reveals that Bora, a protein that accumulates in G2 phase, directly binds to PLK1 and alters its conformation to expose threonine 210 (Thr210 / T210) within its activation loop, dramatically promoting phosphorylation and activation of PLK1-T210 by the kinase Aurora A (AURKA). Cooperative activation of PLK1 by Bora and Aurora A leads to activation of Cdc25C and cyclin B-Cdk1, thereby controlling the G2/M transition (mitotic entry) in somatic cells.

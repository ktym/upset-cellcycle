# Modulation of the mitotic regulatory network by APC-dependent destruction of the Cdh1 inhibitor Acm1

- **PMID**: 18498748
- **Authors**: Maria Enquist-Newman, Meghan Sullivan, David O. Morgan
- **Journal**: Molecular Cell (2008) 30:437-446
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.molcel.2008.04.004
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18498748/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-N20` (`ACM1|BMH1|BMH2` → `CDH1`), `SCE-CC-N36` (`CDC20` → `ACM1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

This study elucidates the mechanism of action of Acm1, an inhibitory protein of the APC/C activator Cdh1 in budding yeast, as well as the proteolytic regulation of Acm1 itself during late mitosis. Acm1 binds with high affinity to the WD40 domain of Cdh1 using conserved D-box and KEN-box motifs, selectively inhibiting APC/C-Cdh1 as a competitive pseudosubstrate. Meanwhile, at anaphase onset, the other APC/C activator, Cdc20 (APC/C-Cdc20), recognizes and ubiquitinates Acm1 as a substrate, targeting it for destruction. Consequently, degradation of Acm1 by APC/C-Cdc20 relieves inhibition of Cdh1, supporting the ordered relay of activation from APC/C-Cdc20 to APC/C-Cdh1.

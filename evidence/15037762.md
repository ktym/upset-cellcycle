# Coupling morphogenesis to mitotic entry

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

For budding yeast to enter mitosis (M phase) in a timely manner, the Cdc28 inhibitory kinase Swe1 (Wee1 ortholog) must be recruited to the septin ring at the bud neck, hyperphosphorylated, and inactivated via ubiquitin-dependent degradation. This study revealed that two protein kinases sequentially localized to the bud neck—the PAK family kinase Cla4 (localized in early S phase) and the Polo-like kinase Cdc5 (localized from late S to G2/M phase)—directly phosphorylate Swe1 in a stepwise manner to trigger its degradation and downregulation. Through this stepwise multisite phosphorylation mechanism, proper formation of the septin ring (morphogenesis) is coupled to mitotic entry.

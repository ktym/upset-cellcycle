## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The cyclin-dependent kinase Cdk2 associates with cyclins A, D, and E and participates in regulating the transition from G1 to S phase in mammals. Using a yeast two-hybrid screen to search for Cdk2-interacting proteins, this study identified a novel 21 kDa protein, p21CIP1 (CDKN1A). p21CIP1 binds tightly to G1 cyclin-CDK complexes, including cyclin A-Cdk2, cyclin E-Cdk2, cyclin D1-Cdk4, and cyclin D2-Cdk4, and potently inhibits their Rb phosphorylation activity, thereby suppressing cell cycle progression.

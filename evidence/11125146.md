- **PMID**: 11125146

- **Journal**: Science (2000) 290:2309-2312
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.290.5500.2309

- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-028` (`GMNN` → `CDT1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In multicellular eukaryotes, ensuring that genomic DNA is replicated once and only once per cell cycle requires strict prevention of replication origin re-firing (re-replication) during S, G2, and M phases. This study revealed that the DNA replication inhibitor Geminin (GMNN) binds tightly and directly to Cdt1 (CDT1), a licensing factor essential for loading the MCM complex onto replication origins. In a cell-free DNA replication system, inhibition of DNA replication by Geminin was completely reversed by the addition of excess Cdt1, and across the cell cycle Cdt1 is present from G1 to S phase while Geminin is expressed from S through G2 and M phases to sequester and inhibit Cdt1. This elucidated the molecular mechanism by which Geminin binds to Cdt1 to prevent inappropriate licensing of replication origins.

# Arrayed BUB recruitment modules in the kinetochore scaffold KNL1 promote accurate chromosome segregation

- **PMID**: 24344183
- **Authors**: Mathijs Vleugel, Eelco Tromer, Manja Omerzu, Vincent Groenewold, Wilco Nijenhuis, Berend Snel, Geert J. P. L. Kops
- **Journal**: Journal of Cell Biology (2013) 203:943-955
- **DOI**: 10.1083/jcb.201307016
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24344183/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N22` (`KNL1` → `BUB1|BUB3`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The kinetochore scaffold protein KNL1 contains an N-terminal array of repeated MELT-like motifs (TxxOmega-MELT sequence modules) phosphorylated by Mps1 kinase. This study reveals that the 19 MELT-like modules in human KNL1 function as autonomous modules that independently recruit the BUB1-BUB3 complex (and BUBR1-BUB3) to kinetochores. While a small number of functional modules suffices to maintain spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) signaling, kinetochore BUB1 accumulation and the efficiency of chromosome alignment to the metaphase plate increase proportionally with the number of active MELT modules, ensuring accurate chromosome segregation.

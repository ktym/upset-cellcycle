# The Swi5 transcription factor of Saccharomyces cerevisiae has a role in exit from mitosis through induction of the cdk-inhibitor Sic1 in telophase

- **PMID**: 9017392
- **Authors**: Toyn JH, Johnson AL, Donovan JD, Toone WM, Johnston LH
- **Journal**: Genetics (1997) 145:85-96
- **DOI**: 10.1093/genetics/145.1.85
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9017392/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-051` (`SWI5` → `SIC1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

In budding yeast, inactivation of M-phase CDK activity is crucial for exit from mitosis in telophase. In this study, analysis of genetic interactions with the Dbf2 protein kinase involved in mitotic exit revealed that the transcription factor Swi5 plays an important role in mitotic exit. Swi5 directly induces transcription of the CDK inhibitor gene SIC1 in telophase, and the SIC1 promoter was shown to contain a Swi5-binding site bound by Swi5 in vitro. This transcriptional induction of SIC1 by Swi5 was demonstrated to contribute to Cdc28 kinase inactivation and mitotic exit in telophase.

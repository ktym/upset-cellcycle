# Phosphorylation of the transcription factor PHO4 by a cyclin-CDK complex, PHO80-PHO85

- **PMID**: 8108735
- **Authors**: A. Kaffman, I. Herskowitz, R. Tjian, E. K. O'Shea
- **Journal**: Science (1994) 263:1153-1156
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.8108735
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8108735/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `SCE-CC-015` (`PHO85|PHO80` → `PHO4`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Transcriptional induction of the acid phosphatase gene PHO5 in budding yeast is mediated by the transcription factors Pho2 and Pho4, which are inactivated by the negative regulators Pho80 and Pho85 under high-phosphate medium conditions. This study revealed that Pho80 shares homology with yeast cyclins and physically forms a complex with the Cdc28-related protein kinase Pho85 (the Pho80-Pho85 cyclin-CDK complex). Furthermore, it demonstrated that the Pho80-Pho85 complex specifically and efficiently phosphorylates the transcription factor Pho4, and that this phosphorylation correlates with the repression of PHO5 transcription under high-phosphate conditions.

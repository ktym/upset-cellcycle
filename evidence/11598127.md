## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

The anaphase-promoting complex (APC/C) is sequentially activated by two activators, Cdc20 and Cdh1 (FZR1), with APC/C-Cdh1 ubiquitinating mitotic cyclins and other substrates to promote mitotic exit. Human Cdh1 (hCdh1) is phosphorylated by cyclin B-Cdc2 (CDK1) during mitosis, altering its conformation and keeping it unable to activate the APC/C. This study revealed that the dual-specificity phosphatase hCdc14a (CDC14A), a human homologue of budding yeast Cdc14 phosphatase, directly dephosphorylates phosphorylated hCdh1 to restore and activate the ubiquitin ligase activity of APC/C-Cdh1. This demonstrated that dephosphorylation of CDH1 by CDC14A drives APC/C-CDH1 activation during mitotic exit in mammalian cells.

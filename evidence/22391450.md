# The MuvB complex sequentially recruits B-Myb and FoxM1 to promote mitotic gene expression

- **PMID**: 22391450
- **Authors**: Subhashini Sadasivam, Shenghua Duan, James A. DeCaprio
- **Journal**: Genes & Development (2012) 26:474-489
- **DOI**: 10.1101/gad.181933.111
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22391450/
- **Related Interaction IDs**: `HSA-CC-N15` (`LIN9|LIN54` → `MYBL2`), `HSA-CC-N16` (`MYBL2|LIN9` → `FOXM1`)

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

During quiescence (G0), the five-subunit MuvB core complex (LIN9, LIN37, LIN52, LIN54, and RBBP4) associates with p130 and E2F4-DP1 to form the DREAM complex and repress cell-cycle genes. This study shows that when cells exit quiescence and enter the cell cycle, the MuvB core dissociates from p130 and binds the transcription factor B-MYB (MYBL2) in S phase to form the MMB (Myb-MuvB) complex, returning to the promoters of late cell-cycle (G2/M) genes. Furthermore, from late S into G2 phase, this MuvB-B-MYB complex recruits the transcription factor FOXM1 to these promoters, and the MuvB-FOXM1 complex remains bound even after proteasomal degradation of B-MYB to drive maximal mitotic gene expression.

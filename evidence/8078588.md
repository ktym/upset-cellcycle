# p15INK4B is a potential effector of TGF-beta-induced cell cycle arrest

- **PMID**: 8078588
- **Authors**: G. J. Hannon, D. Beach
- **Journal**: Nature (1994) 371:257-261
- **DOI**: 10.1038/371257a0

## Summary of Cell-Cycle Regulation

Transforming growth factor-β (TGF-β) inhibits cell proliferation by inducing a cell cycle arrest in G1 phase. In this study, p15INK4B (CDKN2B) was isolated as a novel member of the p16INK4 family that inhibits CDK4 and CDK6. Treatment of human keratinocytes with TGF-β induces p15 expression approximately 30-fold, and p15 binds to CDK4 and CDK6 to inhibit their kinase activity, demonstrating that p15INK4B functions as a key effector of TGF-β-induced G1 arrest.
